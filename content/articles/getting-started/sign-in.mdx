Your HR team adds you to CentaHR, and you get an email inviting you to join. You set a password once; after that, you sign in at [app.centahr.com](https://app.centahr.com/login).

## Accept your invitation

1. Open the email from CentaHR. It comes from noreply@centahr.com, and the subject starts “Invitation to Join” followed by your company's name.
2. Click the link in the email.
3. Your email is already filled in. **Create a password** of at least 8 characters, type it again under **Confirm password**, and click **Activate account**.

The link works once. If it says it has expired or been used, see [the link doesn't work](/troubleshooting/password-reset-token-expired).

## Sign in

1. Go to [app.centahr.com](https://app.centahr.com/login).
2. Enter your **Work email** and **Password**, and click **Log in**.

Where you land depends on who you are:

| You are | You land on |
| --- | --- |
| A new joiner who hasn't finished onboarding | **Onboarding**: a few steps to complete first. See [complete your onboarding](/getting-started/complete-onboarding). |
| An employee | **Home** in the employee portal. See [find your way around](/getting-started/employee-basics). |
| HR or an admin | The dashboard |

## Confirm it's you

If your company has two-step sign-in on, CentaHR emails you a 6-digit code when you haven't signed in for three days, and the first time you sign in.

1. Open the email and type the code under **Verification code**.
2. Click **Verify and continue**.

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your work email, and click **Send reset link**. The link in the email works once, for 24 hours. Then choose a **New password** and click **Update password**.
