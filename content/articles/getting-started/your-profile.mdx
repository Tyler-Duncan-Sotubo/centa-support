| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Your personal details, your family, and your history at the company |
| Employment | Your job role, department, location and line manager, set by HR |
| Payroll | Your pay details and payslips |
| Org chart | Who you report to, and who's in your team |

## Keep it up to date

- **Change what you can.** Cards with **Edit** are yours to update, such as your phone number or address.
- **Ask HR for the rest.** Your job details are set by HR. Tell them if something's wrong.
- **Check before payday.** Make sure your pay details are right before your first payroll.

## Your payslips

Open the **Payroll** tab. Your payslips are listed there once your company has run payroll.
