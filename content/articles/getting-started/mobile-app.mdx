The Centa HR app puts the employee portal on your phone. Your HR team will tell you where to download it.

## Sign in

Use the same **work email** and **password** as on the web. If your company has two-step sign-in, you'll get the same 6-digit code by email. See [sign in for the first time](/getting-started/sign-in).

## What you can do

| For | In the app |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone | Clock in and out, see your timesheet, request leave, see your pay and payslips, update your tasks, write your self review and give feedback |
| Managers | All of that, plus approvals and your team |

## Things to know

- **Allow location.** The app asks for your location when you clock in. Clocking in only works near your workplace. See [clock in and out](/attendance/employee-clock-in-out).
- **Allow notifications** to hear about approvals and reminders.
- **Some things are web only,** such as one-to-ones and objectives. Use the employee portal for those.
