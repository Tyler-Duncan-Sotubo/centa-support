## Home

- **Today:** clock in and out, and see how long you've worked. See [clock in and out](/attendance/employee-clock-in-out).
- **Leave balance:** how much leave you have left.
- **Coming up:** holidays, birthdays and other dates ahead.
- **To do:** new joiners see the steps left to [complete onboarding](/getting-started/complete-onboarding).
- **My team:** if people report to you, who's in today.
- **Quick actions:** Request leave, Claim reimbursement, Salary advance, Request asset, My tasks and Feedback.

## The menu

| Menu | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Approvals | Things waiting for your decision, if you approve anything |
| My team | The people who report to you, if anyone does |
| Profile | Your details, your job and your payslips. See [your profile and payslips](/getting-started/your-profile). |
| Timesheet | Your clock-ins, lateness and absences |
| Leave | Your balances and requests |
| Performance | Your tasks, objectives, one-to-ones, feedback and appraisals. See [what managers and employees see](/performance/managers-and-employees). |
| Salary advance | Ask for part of your pay early |
| Reimbursements | Claim back money you've spent for work |
| Benefits | The benefits your company offers |
| Assets | Equipment you've been given, and requests for more |

Some items only appear if your company uses that part of CentaHR, or if they apply to you.
