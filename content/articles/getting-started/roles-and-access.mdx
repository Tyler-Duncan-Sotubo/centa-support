## The roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Super admin | Full access, including users, roles and system settings |
| Admin | Manage users, assign roles, and oversee HR and payroll |
| HR manager | Look after employee records, hiring and HR tasks |
| Payroll specialist | Handle payroll, tax documents and salary payments |
| Finance officer | Approve reimbursements and pay runs, and see payroll reports |
| Recruiter | Post jobs and look after candidates |
| Line manager | Use the dashboard for the people below them: their timesheets, rota, approvals and reviews |
| Employee | Use the employee portal and the app for their own work |

Everyone also uses the employee portal and the app for their own work, whatever their role: clocking in, leave, payslips, reimbursements, tasks and reviews.

Being someone's manager comes from the reporting line, not the role. Anyone with people under them sees those people's records and approves their requests, whatever their role. Set reporting lines on **Staff → Company → Org chart**.

## Give someone access

- **Someone new:** click **Invite user**, enter their **Name**, **Email address** and **Role**, and click **Send invite**.
- **Change a role:** open the person's menu, choose **Edit**, pick the new **Role** and click **Save**. You can't change your own role.

## Approvals

On the **Approvals** tab:

- **Default manager** approves for anyone without a line manager, and for imported employees with none. Choose someone senior, such as the head of HR. If you don't choose one, those requests go to an HR manager.
- **Waiting approvals** sets when the approver gets a reminder, and when a request escalates to their manager and HR. Requests also escalate straight away if the approver is on leave with no stand-in.
- **When nobody in a role can approve,** HR can. That's a step for a role such as Finance where nobody has the role, they've left, they're on leave with no stand-in, or the only one is the person asking. The request is in HR's **Approvals** straight away and HR gets the reminders. With no HR manager, an admin approves, then the super admin.

## Sign-in protection

- **Two-step sign-in** is on to start with: everyone gets a code by email as a second step when they sign in after three days away. Switch it under **Two-step sign-in**.
- **Security history** lists sign-ins, so you can see who signed in and when.
- **People who leave lose access** the day after their last day, once their offboarding is confirmed under **Staff → Offboarding**.
