When you sign in as a new joiner, CentaHR opens **Onboarding** before anything else. Your HR team chooses the steps; finishing them gives HR what they need to pay you and set you up.

## Work through the steps

1. Sign in with the email and password from your invitation.
2. On **Start Your Journey with Us**, the steps are listed on the left. Start with the first.
3. Fill in each step and click **Next**. Your answers are saved at each step, so you can stop and come back later. **Previous** takes you back a step.
4. On the last step, click **Finish**.

## Things to know

- **Use your own details.** Your bank details are where your salary is paid.
- **Not sure about a step?** Ask your HR team; they can see what you've filled in.
- **Steps left** show on **Home** under **To do** until you've finished them.

Once you're done, see [find your way around the employee portal](/getting-started/employee-basics).
