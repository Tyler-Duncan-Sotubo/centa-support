Setting up CentaHR takes six steps, in this order. The first three are once-only; after that, each module has its own setup checklist and guide.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [app.centahr.com](https://app.centahr.com/login) and click **Create account**.
2. On **Create your account**, enter your first and last name, your **Work email** and a password.
3. On **Tell us about your company**, enter your **Company name** and **Country**.
4. Confirm your email address from the link CentaHR sends you. The link works for 7 days.

You're the first user, with full access. You can give others access later.

## 2. Add your company details

Open your avatar menu at the top right and choose **Company**.

## 3. Add offices, departments and job roles

Under **Staff → Company**:

- **Offices:** your company starts with **Head Office**. Pin it on the map and add any others. Clocking in uses these; see [offices and clock-in locations](/attendance/setup/offices).
- **Departments:** for example Sales, Finance and Operations.
- **Job roles:** the jobs people do, such as Account Executive.

Add these before your employees, so you can choose them as you add people. See [departments and job roles](/staff/company/departments-and-job-roles).

## 4. Add your employees

Add people one at a time, upload a spreadsheet, or invite new joiners to onboard themselves. See [add your employees](/getting-started/add-employees).

## 5. Give people the right access

Decide who manages what, set line managers, and choose a default manager. See [roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## 6. Set up each module

Each module in the menu has a **Setup checklist** that leads you through its settings. Guides so far:

- [Staff](/staff/overview): employee records, the org chart, onboarding and offboarding
- [Performance](/performance/overview): appraisals, tasks and feedback
- [Attendance](/attendance/overview): clocking in, shifts and timesheets
- [Leave](/leave/overview): leave types, requests, approvals and balances
- [Payroll](/payroll/overview): pay runs, payslips, remittances and salary advances

The checklist leaves the menu once you've worked through it.
