There are three ways to add people. Each one emails the person an invitation to set their password and sign in.

| Way | Use it for | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add employee | One person at a time, with all their details | **Staff → Employees → Add employee** |
| Upload employees | Many existing staff at once, from a CSV file | **Staff → Employees → Upload employees** |
| Onboarding invite | New joiners, who fill in their own details | **Staff → Onboarding → Send onboarding invite** |

## 1. Add one person

1. On **Staff → Employees**, click **Add employee**.
2. Fill in their details: basic information, their work (job role, department and location), personal information, pay, and any dependents.
3. Click **Submit**. They get an email titled “Invitation to Join” followed by your company's name and their role.
4. Give them a line manager: on their profile's **Employment** tab, click **Change Manager**.

Every field is explained in [add and import employees](/staff/people/add-and-import).

## 2. Upload many at once

1. On **Staff → Employees**, click **Upload employees**.
2. Click **Download Example** and fill it in, one person per row.
3. Drag the file in, or click to choose it, and click **Add**.

Each person in the file is added and gets an invitation email. See [getting the file right](/staff/people/add-and-import#getting-the-file-right) before you upload.

## 3. Invite a new joiner

For someone starting soon, send an onboarding invite instead. They fill in their own details through your onboarding steps before their first day.

1. Go to **Staff → Onboarding** and fill in **Send onboarding invite**.
2. Click **Send invite**.

They'll see your onboarding steps when they first sign in. See [complete your onboarding](/getting-started/complete-onboarding).

## Before people sign in

- **Set each person's location and line manager.** Clocking in and approvals depend on them.
- **Check the email addresses.** Invitations go to the address you entered.
- **Tell people to expect the email,** from noreply@centahr.com. See [didn't get an email](/troubleshooting/emails-not-arriving) if it doesn't arrive.
