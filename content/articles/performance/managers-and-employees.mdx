Employees and managers work in the employee portal on the web and in the **Centa HR** mobile app. Nobody needs HR access to take part: in CentaHR, a manager is anyone with people under them in the reporting line, whatever their role.

Share this page with your managers and employees when you launch Performance.

## Employees

In the web portal, **Performance** (under Growth) has five pages.

| Page | What they do there |
| --- | --- |
| Tasks | Add their own tasks (these wait for their manager's approval), post progress updates, comment and attach files |
| Objectives | See the company and team objectives for the cycle, and their progress |
| One-to-ones | See upcoming meetings, add agenda items, and keep shared and private notes |
| Feedback | Give 360° feedback, and read feedback shared with them |
| Appraisals | Write their self review and see their completed appraisals |

### Write a self review

1. Go to **Performance → Appraisals** and click **New appraisal**.
2. Pick the cycle.
3. Click **Start assessment**, then fill in the tabs: **Values**, **Tasks** and **Summary**.
4. Click **Submit assessment**. Submitting locks it.

They can read their manager's conclusion once the manager completes their appraisal.

The mobile app covers self reviews, tasks (with progress updates and attachments) and 360° feedback. One-to-ones and objectives are on the web portal only.

## Managers

Managers get everything employees get, plus **My team**: everyone who reports to them, today's attendance, and a menu of actions for each person.

- **My team** lists everyone who reports to them, with today's attendance: in today, late, on leave and not clocked in. They can switch between **Direct reports** and **Everyone under me**.
- **Each person's menu** offers **Assign a task**, **Schedule a one-to-one**, **Give feedback**, and **Start a review** or **Open review**.
- **Approvals** holds the tasks their people create for themselves, waiting for a decision.
- **Calibration** appears on My team for managers of managers.
- **The weekly digest**, if HR switches it on, arrives on Monday mornings.

On the mobile app, managers also see their team's tasks, reviews and feedback.

## A short brief for managers

Copy this into your launch email:

1. **Set tasks in the first week of the cycle**, with weights that add up to 100 for each person, and link them to an objective.
2. **Approve tasks** your people add for themselves, from **Approvals**.
3. **Hold one-to-ones** at least monthly, from **My team**. Use the agenda, and link items to tasks.
4. **When appraisals open**, start each review from **My team**, answer every question, rate every task, and write the conclusion.
5. **If your score differs from the one the task ratings suggest**, say why. Then click **Complete review**: it locks.
