## Someone has no appraisal in the current cycle

Automatic appraisals skip a person when any of these is true:

- **Create reviews automatically** is off ([step 1](/performance/setup/general-settings)).
- No cycle covers today, or the default template was deleted.
- Their status isn't Active or Probation. People on leave are skipped.
- They have no line manager with a login, and no default manager is set.

Appraisals are added overnight, up to 100 people a night. To add someone now, open the cycle and click **New Review** ([step 5](/performance/setup/first-cycle#2-add-people)).

## An appraisal went to the wrong manager

The reviewer is the person's line manager when the appraisal is created. Fix the reporting line first ([before you start](/performance/before-you-start#set-reporting-lines)) so new appraisals go to the right person. To move an appraisal that already exists, [contact support](/contact).

## An employee can't write a self review

- They need a CentaHR login.
- Each person can have one self review per cycle. If one exists, they open it from **Performance → Appraisals**.
- The cycle has to exist. Check **Performance → Appraisals**.

## There's no suggested score

The suggested score comes from task ratings. Check that:

- the appraisal's template includes **Tasks** ([step 3](/performance/setup/review-templates));
- the person has tasks in this cycle that are **Active** or **Completed**;
- the manager has rated at least one task.

## A task isn't on the appraisal

Only tasks in the appraisal's cycle that are **Active** or **Completed** appear. Drafts, tasks waiting for approval and archived tasks don't. Check the task's **Review cycle** and **Status** on its edit page.

## A recurring task didn't appear in the new cycle

It's copied overnight after the new cycle starts, as a **Draft**. Drafts aren't shown to the owner, so the manager needs to check it and set it to **Active**.

## The manager can't complete the review

- Only the reviewer can complete it. HR can start a review on the reviewer's behalf, but not answer it.
- **Final summary**, **Strengths** and **Areas for improvement** all need text.
- If the final score differs from the one the task ratings suggest, the manager has to say why.

## A completed review needs changing

Completed reviews are locked, so their answers and conclusion can't change. To change a manager review's score, use **Adjust score** on [Calibration](/performance/use/calibration); a reason is required, and the original is kept.

## Calibration says “Not available” or “Nothing to compare yet”

- **Not available:** calibration is for HR and for managers of managers.
- **Nothing to compare yet:** only completed manager reviews appear. Self and peer reviews never do.

## Nobody gets check-in reminders

- Reminders go to task owners who have a CentaHR login.
- They go out on the anchor day and hour set in your task policies, in your company's time zone.
- Each task keeps the schedule it was created with, so policy changes only affect new tasks ([step 4](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback)).

## People can't see some kinds of feedback in Give feedback

The menu only lists kinds switched on for their group in [feedback settings](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback#feedback-settings), and nothing at all if 360° feedback is switched off for the group. Employees can be given Self, Peer and Employee to manager; Manager to employee is for managers only, and starts switched off.

## Someone isn't in the list when giving feedback

That kind of feedback may be limited to the sender's own office or department in [feedback settings](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback#feedback-settings). Employees' peer feedback starts limited to the same office.

## Send anonymously is missing

**Allow Anonymous 360 Feedback** is switched off in [feedback settings](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback#feedback-settings).

## The cycle length is wrong

Change **Cycle length** in [general settings](/performance/setup/general-settings). The current cycle keeps its dates, so edit them from the cycle's menu under **Performance → Appraisals**. Don't delete a cycle that has appraisals or tasks: everything in it is deleted too.

## HR can't see one-to-ones

That's by design. Only the manager and the employee in a one-to-one can open it.
