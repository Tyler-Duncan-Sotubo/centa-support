Task policies decide when people are reminded to update their tasks. Feedback settings decide which kinds of 360° feedback people can give, and what they're asked. Both start with workable defaults, so the one change most companies need is the time zone.

## Task policies

Go to **Performance → Settings → Task policies**, change what you need and click **Save company policy**.

| | Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Default visibility | Who can see a new task: **Company** (anyone in the company), **Manager** (the owner and the managers above them) or **Private** (only the owner and HR). | Company |
| 2 | Check-in cadence | How often a task's owner is due to update its progress: Weekly, Bi-weekly or Monthly. | Monthly |
| 3 | Timezone | The local time zone check-ins are scheduled in. | Europe/London |
| 4 | Anchor weekday and Anchor hour | The day and hour a check-in falls due, and the reminder goes out. | Monday, 9:00 |

- **Set your own time zone**, for example Africa/Lagos. The default is Europe/London.
- **Policies apply to new tasks.** Each task keeps the schedule it was created with, so set this before managers start giving out tasks.
- **Owners get an email when a check-in is due**, on the anchor day and hour in your time zone, titled “Check-in:” and the task's name.

## Feedback settings

Go to **Performance → Settings → Feedback settings**. The page has two tabs: **Feedback Settings** and **Feedback Questions**.

There are four kinds of 360° feedback. Each one is switched on or off separately for employees and for managers, and each change saves at once.

| Kind | What it's for | Employees | Managers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self | Reflecting on your own work | On | On |
| Peer | Feedback between colleagues | On | On |
| Employee → Manager | Feedback for your manager | On | On |
| Manager → Employee | A manager's feedback for someone they manage | Not offered | Off |

- **Turn Manager → Employee on for managers** if they should give feedback outside the appraisal. It's off to start with.
- **These switches decide what's in the Give feedback menu.** HR and admins can always send every kind. People with the Line manager role get the manager choices; everyone else gets the employee choices.
- **Enable 360 Feedback for Employees** and **Enable 360 Feedback for Managers** switch 360° feedback on or off for the whole group. Within a group, each kind has its own switch.
- **Restrict to same office** and **Restrict to same department** limit who people can send that kind of feedback to. To start with, employees' peer feedback is limited to their own office, and feedback for managers to the manager's own department.
- **Allow Anonymous 360 Feedback** lets people leave their name off. Switch it off if every piece of feedback should carry a name.

On the **Feedback Questions** tab, each kind starts with three questions, such as “Where does this person perform well?” and “How can this person improve?”. Add your own, choose an input type (Text, Rating, Yes / No, Dropdown or Checkbox), and drag questions into the order you want.
