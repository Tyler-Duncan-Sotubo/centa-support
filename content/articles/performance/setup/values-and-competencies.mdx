Competencies are the qualities you appraise people on, and each one holds questions. Your company starts with 13 built-in competencies and 24 built-in questions, so you can run a first cycle without writing any. In an appraisal, these questions appear under the heading **Values**, grouped by competency.

Go to **Performance → Settings → Values & competencies**. Competencies are listed on the left; the questions for the one you select are on the right.

The built-in competencies are Accountability, Attendance, Communication, Customer Service, Job Knowledge, Leadership, Problem Solving, Productivity, Quality of Work, Self-Development, Skills, Teamwork and Technical. They're shared by every company on CentaHR, so they and their questions can't be edited or deleted. Accountability, Attendance and Skills have no built-in questions.

## Add your company values

Add each of your company values as a competency, so appraisals ask about them by name.

1. Click **Add Competency**.
2. Enter a **Name** (for example, Ownership) and a one-line **Description**. Names must be unique in your company.
3. Save. Competencies you create can be edited or deleted from the list.

## Add questions

1. Select a competency on the left.
2. Click **Add Question**.
3. Write the question and choose its **Type**.
4. Choose the **Competency** it belongs to, and click **Create**.

| Type | What the reviewer sees | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rating | 1 to 5 stars | How well or how often something happens |
| Yes / No | Two buttons | A clear standard that is met or not |
| Text | A text box | Examples and evidence |
| Dropdown | A list of choices, one per line under **Choices** (at least two) | One answer from a fixed set |

Write questions about behaviour a manager can observe, such as “Does the employee meet deadlines consistently?”. Aim for 8 to 15 questions per appraisal: more than that and answers get thin.

Switch on **Is Mandatory** for a question that must be answered before the appraisal can be completed. It's marked **Required** on the review, and the review can't be submitted or completed until it's answered. Switch on **Allow Notes** to let the reviewer add a note to their answer.

## Optional: expected levels per job role

The **Appraisal Framework** item on the Setup checklist opens **Performance Framework**. There you can record the level each job role should reach in each competency: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Proficient or Expert / Leader.

1. Pick a job role on the left. Job roles come from **Staff → Company → Job roles**.
2. Click **Add Expectation**, choose a competency and its **Expected Level**, and save.

Expected levels show managers the standard each role should meet in each competency.
