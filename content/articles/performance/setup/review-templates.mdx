A template decides which sections an appraisal has and which questions it asks. Your company starts with one template, **Default Performance Review**, and automatic appraisals always use it. Edit it to suit you rather than deleting it.

Go to **Performance → Settings → Templates**. The table shows each template, which one is the default, and which sections it includes.

## What a template can include

| Option | What it does | Default Performance Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Questionnaire | The template's questions under **Values**, grouped by competency. Answers save as the reviewer goes. | On, with 8 built-in questions |
| Tasks | The person's active and completed tasks in the cycle, with weight, due date and progress. The reviewer rates each task from 1 to 5 stars, and the ratings suggest a final score. | Off |
| Feedback | 360° feedback the person received between the cycle's start and end dates. Anonymous feedback stays anonymous. | Off |
| Attendance | Days present, absent and late during the cycle, from clock-ins. | On |
| Require Signature | The employee and the reviewer must both sign the final report. | On |
| Restrict Visibility | Only HR and the person's direct manager can view the appraisal. | Off |

Every appraisal also ends with a **Conclusion**: a final summary, strengths, areas for improvement, a final score out of 100, and a recommendation of Promote, Hold or Exit.

<Callout type="tip" title="Switch on Tasks if managers set tasks">

Without **Tasks**, appraisals have no task ratings and no suggested score. Open the default template's menu, choose **Edit**, tick **Tasks**, and save. Tick **Feedback** too once you use 360° feedback.

</Callout>

## Create a template

1. Click **New Template**.
2. Give it a **Name** (for example, Mid-Year Review) and a short **Description**.
3. Under **Options**, tick the sections it includes.
4. Click **Create**. If you ticked Questionnaire, you're asked **Add questions now?** Click **Add questions**.
5. Tick the questions to include and save.

To change a template's questions later, open its menu and choose **Edit questions**. To change its name or sections, choose **Edit**.

## Before you edit a template

- **Edit between cycles where you can.** Open appraisals read their template live, so a question you remove also disappears from appraisals in progress.
- **Keep a default template.** Automatic appraisals always use the template marked **Default**, so edit Default Performance Review rather than deleting it.
- **Other templates are for appraisals you add by hand.** When you create an appraisal yourself, you choose its template ([step 5](/performance/setup/first-cycle)).
- **Who can see an appraisal:** HR, the reviewer and the managers above the person. Switch on **Restrict Visibility** to limit it to HR and the person's direct manager. See [roles and glossary](/performance/roles-and-glossary).
