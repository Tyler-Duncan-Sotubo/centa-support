A cycle is a dated period, such as H1 2026, that holds everyone's appraisals and tasks. With the default settings, the cycle and its manager appraisals create themselves. Your job is to check them, add self reviews if you want them, and follow up until every appraisal is submitted.

## 1. Create the cycle

If **Start cycles automatically** is on, the current cycle already exists: check its name and dates under **Performance → Appraisals**. Otherwise:

1. Go to **Performance → Appraisals** and click **Create Appraisal Cycle**.
2. Enter a **Name**, a **Start Date** and an **End Date**.
3. Click **Create**. A cycle that has started shows as Active; one that starts later shows as Draft.

Two cycles can't share a name or overlap. Make the dates cover the whole period you're appraising: tasks belong to a cycle, and the Feedback and Attendance sections only count what happened between its dates.

<Callout type="danger" title="Deleting a cycle deletes everything in it">

Its appraisals, tasks and objectives go with it, and they can't be brought back. To fix a cycle's name or dates, use **Edit** from its menu instead.

</Callout>

## 2. Add people

**Automatically.** With **Create reviews automatically** on, a manager appraisal appears overnight for each active or probation employee, addressed to their line manager. New joiners are picked up the night after they join.

**By hand.** Open the cycle and click **New Review**. Choose the **Employee**, the **Performance Cycle**, the **Template** and the **Review Type**, then click **Create**. Each person can have one review of each type per cycle.

## 4. How a manager completes an appraisal

1. Open the appraisal and click **Start review**.
2. Work through the tabs: **Values** (answer each question), **Tasks** (rate each task from 1 to 5 stars), **Feedback** and **Attendance**. Each tab has its own comment box.
3. On **Conclusion**, write the **Final summary**, **Strengths** and **Areas for improvement**. **Suggest with Centa AI** can draft them for the manager to edit.
4. Set the **Final score** out of 100. If tasks were rated, CentaHR shows the score they suggest. A different score needs a reason.
5. Choose a recommendation: **Promote**, **Hold** or **Exit**.
6. Click **Save draft** at any point. When it's finished, click **Complete review**. It locks: nobody can edit the answers or the conclusion after this.

## 5. Finish the cycle

When the manager appraisals are submitted, compare scores across teams on [Calibration](/performance/use/calibration). Then use [Reports](/performance/use/reports) for the results.

Appraisals can still be completed after the end date. If cycles start automatically, the next one is created the night after this one ends.
