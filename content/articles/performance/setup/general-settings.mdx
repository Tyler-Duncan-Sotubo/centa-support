These five settings decide when review cycles start and whether appraisals create themselves. A new company starts with quarterly cycles and automatic appraisals switched on, so check them before your first cycle begins.

Go to **Performance → Settings → General settings**. Each change saves as soon as you make it.

| | Setting | What it does | New company default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Start cycles automatically | When a cycle ends, the next one is created overnight. If there's no cycle yet, the first one covers today. | On |
| 2 | Cycle length | Monthly, Quarterly, Every six months or Yearly. Automatic cycles are named like FEB-2026, Q1 2026, H1 2026 or Annual 2026. | Quarterly |
| 3 | Team result share (%) | For a task shared by a team, how much of each person's score comes from the team's result. The rest comes from their own contribution. Any whole number from 0 to 100. | 50% |
| 4 | Create reviews automatically | Every night, adds a manager appraisal in the current cycle for each active or probation employee who doesn't have one, and sends it to their line manager. | On |
| 5 | Weekly digest | A Monday-morning email to every manager with direct reports: what's waiting for them, overdue tasks, who's off this week and what's coming up (probation ends, birthdays, work anniversaries). Managers with nothing to report get no email. | Off |

## Things to know

- **Cycle length applies from the next cycle.** The current cycle keeps its dates.
- **Automatic appraisals need two things:** a cycle that covers today, and a default review template (see [step 3](/performance/setup/review-templates)). Without either, nothing is created.
- **People without a line manager go to your default manager.** With no default manager, they're skipped. See [before you start](/performance/before-you-start#choose-a-default-manager).
- **Automatic appraisals add up to 100 people a night.** A company larger than that is covered over a few nights.
- **Prefer to choose who is appraised?** Turn off **Create reviews automatically** and add people to the cycle yourself ([step 5](/performance/setup/first-cycle)).
- **Prefer your own cycle names and dates?** Turn off **Start cycles automatically** and create each cycle by hand. Two cycles can't share a name or overlap in dates.

<Callout type="warning" title="A quarterly cycle already exists, but you appraise twice a year">

Change **Cycle length** to **Every six months**, then go to **Performance → Appraisals**, open the current cycle's menu and choose **Edit** to set the dates you want. Don't delete the cycle once it has reviews or tasks in it: deleting a cycle deletes everything in it.

</Callout>
