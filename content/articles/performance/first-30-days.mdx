A four-week plan to go from switching Performance on to a cycle that runs itself. It assumes you appraise at the end of the cycle; move week 4 to the last month of the cycle if your cycle is longer.

## Week 1: data and settings

- [ ] Fix reporting lines, set a default manager and give roles ([before you start](/performance/before-you-start)).
- [ ] Choose your cycle length and decide whether appraisals create themselves ([step 1](/performance/setup/general-settings)).
- [ ] Add your company values as competencies, with two or three questions each ([step 2](/performance/setup/values-and-competencies)).
- [ ] Edit Default Performance Review: switch on Tasks, and Feedback if you'll use it, and choose its questions ([step 3](/performance/setup/review-templates)).
- [ ] Set your time zone and check-in cadence, and choose the kinds of feedback people can give ([step 4](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback)).

## Week 2: objectives and tasks

- [ ] Set three to five company objectives for the cycle ([objectives](/performance/use/objectives)).
- [ ] Ask managers to set team objectives under them.
- [ ] Send managers the [short brief](/performance/managers-and-employees#a-short-brief-for-managers) and ask them to give out tasks with weights.
- [ ] Tell employees they can add their own tasks for approval.

## Week 3: habits

- [ ] Check that every active employee has at least one task: **Performance → Tasks**, and the Tasks card on the Overview.
- [ ] Ask managers to book their first one-to-ones from **My team**.
- [ ] Switch on the managers' weekly digest ([step 1](/performance/setup/general-settings)).
- [ ] Invite everyone to send one piece of 360° feedback.

## Week 4: ready for appraisals

- [ ] Open the cycle under **Performance → Appraisals** and check that everyone has a manager appraisal with the right reviewer.
- [ ] Add appraisals by hand for anyone missing, such as people on leave.
- [ ] Decide whether you want self reviews, and tell employees how to write one.
- [ ] Book a calibration meeting for the week after appraisals are due.

## At the end of the cycle

1. Managers complete their appraisals; chase from the **Not Started** and **In Progress** tabs.
2. HR and senior managers calibrate the scores ([calibration](/performance/use/calibration)).
3. Employees read their conclusions, and you take results from the cycle page and Calibration.
4. The next cycle starts on its own, and recurring tasks come back as drafts for managers to check.
