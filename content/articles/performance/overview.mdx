Performance covers the whole loop. At the start of a cycle you set objectives and tasks; during it, people track progress, meet one-to-one and give feedback; at the end, managers appraise, HR calibrates, and the results go into reports.

These guides are for the HR team setting Performance up. If you're a manager or an employee, start with [what managers and employees see](/performance/managers-and-employees).

## How a cycle runs

A cycle is a dated period, such as H1 2026, that holds everyone's tasks and appraisals. Every cycle runs through the same seven stages.

<CycleFlow />

## The parts of Performance

| Part | What it is | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Appraisals](/performance/setup/first-cycle) | One review per person per cycle. The manager review holds the final score out of 100 and a recommendation; employees can add a self review. | HR runs the cycle, managers write reviews, employees write self reviews |
| [Tasks](/performance/use/tasks) | What a person or team should deliver in the cycle, each with a weight. Managers rate them in the appraisal. | Managers assign them, employees update them |
| [Objectives](/performance/use/objectives) | The company's and teams' aims for the cycle. Task progress rolls up to them. | HR sets company objectives, managers set team ones |
| [One-to-ones](/performance/use/one-to-ones) | Manager–employee meetings with an agenda and notes | A manager and their report; HR and managers above can read them |
| [360° feedback](/performance/use/feedback) | Feedback between colleagues, and to and from managers, at any time | Everyone |
| [Calibration](/performance/use/calibration) | Comparing finished scores across teams, and adjusting them with a reason | HR, and managers of managers |
| [Reports](/performance/use/reports) | The Overview page and exportable reports | HR |

CentaHR uses **appraisal** and **review** for the same thing, and **task** for what other systems call a goal.

## Set it up in five steps

First check [your people data](/performance/before-you-start): reporting lines decide who appraises whom. Then work through the steps in order.

<SetupSteps />

Each step matches an item on **Performance → Setup checklist**. Clicking an item there opens its page and ticks it off. The checklist leaves the menu once all seven items are ticked.

| Step | Setup checklist item |
| --- | --- |
| 1. General settings | General Settings |
| 2. Values and competencies | Competency Framework, and Appraisal Framework if you record expected levels per job role |
| 3. Review templates | Performance Templates |
| 4. Task policies and feedback | Task Policies and Feedback Settings |
| 5. Your first cycle | Start 1:1 Check-in, which opens Appraisals |

<Callout type="tip" title="In a hurry?">

The defaults already work. Once reporting lines are set, a quarterly cycle and its manager appraisals start on their own, using the default template. Steps 2 to 4 make them fit your company.

</Callout>
