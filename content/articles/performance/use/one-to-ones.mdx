One-to-ones are regular meetings between a manager and someone who reports to them, with an agenda and notes. Only the two people in a one-to-one can open it, HR included, so people can speak freely.

## Schedule one

1. Go to **Performance → One-to-ones**, or open **My team** and choose **Schedule a one-to-one** from a person's menu.
2. Click **Schedule**.
3. Choose who it's **With** and **When**, and click **Schedule**.

Anything left open on the agenda from your last one-to-one carries over.

- **Either person** can add agenda items, tick them off, and link an item to one of the employee's current tasks.
- **Shared notes** are seen by both people. **Private notes** are only ever seen by their author.
- **Only the manager** can cancel the meeting or mark it as done.

## What HR can see

Nothing: one-to-ones don't appear for HR or in reports. To encourage them, include them in your [first 30 days plan](/performance/first-30-days) and ask managers to book them from **My team**.
