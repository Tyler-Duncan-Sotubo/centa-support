One-to-ones are regular meetings between a manager and someone who reports to them, with an agenda and notes. Only the two people in a one-to-one can change it. HR, and the managers above the lead, can read it, but never anyone's private notes.

## Schedule one

1. Go to **Performance → One-to-ones**, or open **My team** and choose **Schedule a one-to-one** from a person's menu.
2. Click **Schedule**.
3. Choose who it's **With** and **When**, and click **Schedule**.

Anything left open on the agenda from your last one-to-one carries over. The person you're meeting gets an email with a calendar invite, so it lands in their calendar.

Your upcoming one-to-ones also show under **Coming up** on **My team**, and open from there.

## Find one

**Performance → One-to-ones** lists them in a table:

- **Upcoming**, **Done**, **Cancelled** or **All**. Upcoming ones run soonest first; the rest, newest first.
- A month, or a review cycle, from the menu beside the search.
- Search by name.
- If you can read others' one-to-ones, switch between **Mine** and **Across my team**.

- **Either person** can add agenda items, tick them off, and link an item to one of the employee's current tasks.
- **Shared notes** are seen by both people. **Private notes** are only ever seen by their author.
- **Only the manager** can change the time, cancel the meeting or mark it as done. Click **Change time** to move it: the employee is told, and the invite in their calendar moves too.

## Who can see them

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| The manager and the employee | Everything above. Each keeps their own private notes. |
| HR and admins | Read every one-to-one in the company: when it is, the agenda and the shared notes |
| Managers higher up | Read the one-to-ones held by leads anywhere under them, in the same way |

People who can read a one-to-one find it under **Across my team** on **Performance → One-to-ones**. They can't add to it or change it, and nobody but the author ever sees private notes.

To encourage one-to-ones, include them in your [first 30 days plan](/performance/first-30-days) and ask managers to book them from **My team**.

## Emails and reminders

| When | Who hears | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| It's booked | The employee | Email with a calendar invite, and a notification |
| It's moved or cancelled | The other person | Email, with the calendar invite updated or removed, and a notification |
| The day before | Both of you | Email and a notification at 8am |
| 30 minutes before | Both of you | Email and a notification |
| Something's added to the agenda | The other person | A notification |
| It's marked as done | Both of you | Email with the shared notes and anything still open |

Nobody is told about something they did themselves, and nothing goes to people who have left.
