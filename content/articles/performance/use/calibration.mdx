Calibration makes one manager's 80 mean the same as another's. Run it once the manager appraisals in a cycle are complete, before you share results or use them for pay and promotion decisions.

Go to **Performance → Calibration** and pick the cycle. Only completed manager appraisals appear.

## Read the page

- **Score spread** counts finished appraisals in five bands: under 60, 60–69, 70–79, 80–89 and 90–100.
- **Average by reviewer** shows each manager's average score. Look for anyone far above or below the rest: in the drawing, Ngozi Obi scores 14 points above the next reviewer.

## Adjust a score

1. Open the person's row menu and choose **Adjust score** (marked 1 above).
2. Enter the new **Score out of 100**.
3. Under **Why**, say why it's changing. A reason is required.
4. Save. The new score is the one everyone sees, and the reviewer's original is kept.

To undo an adjustment, choose **Put back the original** from the row menu.

## Who can calibrate

- **HR** can calibrate everyone.
- **A manager of managers** can calibrate the people in their own line. Managers whose reports have no reports of their own see “Calibration is for HR, and for managers of managers.”
- **Nobody can change their own score.**

Only the score changes. The appraisal's answers and conclusion stay locked, as the reviewer left them.
