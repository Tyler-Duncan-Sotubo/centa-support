Tasks are what someone is expected to deliver in a cycle; other systems call them goals. They feed straight into the appraisal score, so start them on the first day of the cycle.

Go to **Performance → Tasks**. The tabs are **Active**, **Incomplete**, **Completed**, **Overdue** and **Archived**.

## Create a task

Click **New task** and fill in the form.

| | Field | What to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Owned by | **An employee** or **A team**. Teams come from **Staff → Teams**. This can't be changed after the task is created. |
| 2 | How the team works on it | **One shared task** gives the whole team one progress bar. **A separate copy for each person** gives everyone their own. |
| 3 | Objective | The objective the task contributes to. Its progress rolls up there. |
| 4 | Repeat each cycle | Copies the task into the next cycle when this one ends, as a draft for the manager to check and make active. |
| 5 | Weight | 0 to 100: how much the task counts in the appraisal, compared with the person's other tasks. |

Choose **No cycle: set the dates** under **Review cycle** to give a task its own dates instead.

## Who can create tasks

- **HR and admins:** for anyone.
- **Managers:** for anyone below them in the reporting line. A team task needs everyone in the team to report to them.
- **Employees:** for themselves. Their task waits in **Approvals** for their line manager (or HR) to approve. Rejecting one needs a reason.

## Progress, check-ins and comments

Owners add progress updates (a percentage and an optional note), comments and attachments. When a check-in falls due under your [task policies](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback), CentaHR emails the owner a reminder.

A completed task can't be edited. A task with any activity can't be deleted, only archived.

## How tasks are scored in the appraisal

If the template includes Tasks, the appraisal lists the person's **active** and **completed** tasks in the cycle. Drafts, tasks waiting for approval and archived tasks don't appear.

- **Each star is worth 20 points**, so 4 stars counts as 80 out of 100.
- **The suggested score is the weighted average** of the rated tasks. If no rated task has a weight, each counts the same.
- **A shared task gets two ratings:** **Team result**, one rating for everyone on the team, and **Their contribution**, for this person alone. Your **Team result share** ([step 1](/performance/setup/general-settings)) decides the blend. At the default 50%, 4 stars for the team and 5 for their contribution scores 90.

In the drawing, the suggested score is (80 × 30 + 60 × 20 + 90 × 50) ÷ 100 = 81.
