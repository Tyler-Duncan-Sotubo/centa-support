Objectives are what the company or a team is trying to achieve in a cycle. Tasks link to them, and their progress rolls up. Set company objectives before managers set tasks, so every task can link to one.

Go to **Performance → Objectives** and pick the cycle.

## Add an objective

1. Click **Company objective** (HR) or **Team objective** (managers).
2. Enter a **Title** and, if you like, a **Description**.
3. For a team objective, choose the company objective it **Supports**, or **No company objective**.
4. Click **Add objective**. Managers can now link tasks to it from the task form.

## How progress is worked out

- **An objective's progress is the plain average** of the tasks linked to it and the team objectives under it.
- **A team task counts once**, however many people share it.
- **Until a task is linked**, the objective shows “No tasks linked yet”, and it doesn't pull its parent's progress down.

## Who can do what

- **Company objectives** are set by HR.
- **Team objectives** are set by managers.
- **Only its creator or HR** can change or archive an objective. Archive one from its row menu.
