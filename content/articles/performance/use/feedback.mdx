360° feedback can be sent at any time, outside the appraisal: about yourself, to a colleague, to your manager, or by a manager to someone they manage. Which kinds people can send is set in [feedback settings](/performance/setup/task-policies-and-feedback#feedback-settings).

## Give feedback

1. Go to **Performance → 360° feedback** and click **Give feedback**.
2. Choose the kind: **Self**, **Peer**, **Employee to manager** or **Manager to employee**.
3. Choose the **Employee** it's about. Your company can limit some kinds of feedback to people in your own office or department.
4. Choose **Who can see it**.
5. Turn on **Send anonymously** if your name shouldn't show. It's there when your company allows anonymous feedback.
6. Answer the questions and click **Send feedback**.

## Who can see it

The sender chooses, with the setting marked 1:

| Who can see it | Who that means |
| --- | --- |
| Only them | The person it's about |
| Only their managers | Their line manager, not the person |
| Them and their managers | The person and their line manager |
| Everyone in their department | Everyone in the person's department |

Anonymous feedback shows as from “Anonymous” in feedback lists, reports and appraisals.

## Feedback in the appraisal

If the appraisal's template includes **Feedback** ([step 3](/performance/setup/review-templates)), everything the person received between the cycle's start and end dates appears on its Feedback tab. Archived feedback is left out.

The 360° feedback page lists feedback by kind, with an **Archived** tab, and can be filtered by department. HR sees all of it; managers see feedback about the people in their line.
