- **Assessments:** the average score, how many are submitted, in progress or not started, and the recommendations made.
- **Tasks:** how many are due this cycle, the share completed, and how many are overdue or open.
- **Feedback:** how much has been given, per person and by kind.
- **Top performer:** the highest final score this cycle.

## Reports

Go to **Performance → Reports**. Filter by cycle, employee, department and type, then export as CSV or PDF.

| Report | What it answers | Columns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task Achievement Report | Who has what to deliver, and whether it's on track | Employee, department, task title, type, status, weight, start and due dates, description |
| Feedback Report | Who is giving and receiving 360° feedback | Employee, kind, anonymous or not, sender, date submitted, number of responses |
| Appraisal Summary Report | Each person's appraisal score and promotion recommendation | Employee, department, score, promotion, date submitted, appraisal note |
| Competency Heatmap Report | How many people sit at each level of each competency | Competency, level, count |

While a cycle is running, the cycle page under **Performance → Appraisals** shows each person's status and score, and [Calibration](/performance/use/calibration) shows the spread of finished scores.

Reports are for HR: people with the Super admin, Admin or HR manager role.
