CentaHR's emails come from **noreply@centahr.com**. Most missing emails are in a spam folder, or went to a different address.

## The emails you might be waiting for

| Email | When it's sent | How long it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation, titled “Invitation to Join” and your company's name | When HR adds you | Until you use it |
| Password reset | When you click **Forgot password?** | 24 hours, once |
| Sign-in code | When two-step sign-in asks for one | 10 minutes |

## Find it

1. **Check spam, junk and promotions folders.**
2. **Search your inbox** for “CentaHR” or “noreply@centahr.com”.
3. **Check the address.** Invitations go to the address HR entered. Ask them to check it on your profile.
4. **Wait a few minutes,** then ask for it again. For reset links and codes, only the newest one works.

## Work email blocking it?

Some company email systems hold back messages from outside senders. Ask whoever manages your company's email to allow **noreply@centahr.com**.

## For HR admins

- **Check the person's email address** on their profile under **Staff → Employees**. A typo means the invitation went nowhere.
- **Send a new invitation** once the address is right.
- **Tell people to expect the email,** so they look for it.

Still not arriving? [Contact support](/contact) with the email address and roughly when it should have arrived.
