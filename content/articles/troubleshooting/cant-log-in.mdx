| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invalid email or password | There's no account with that email address. | Use your work email, exactly as your HR team entered it. If you've never set a password, find your invitation email first. |
| Invalid credentials | The password is wrong, or your account doesn't have access to this part of CentaHR. | [Reset your password](#reset-your-password). If that doesn't help, ask your HR team to check your role. |
| Your access ended when you left. | Your employment has ended, so your account is closed. | Contact your former employer's HR team if you need payslips or documents. |

If you're asked for a code after your password, see [the sign-in code doesn't work](/troubleshooting/sign-in-code).

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your work email and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the newest email from CentaHR, click the link, choose a **New password**, and click **Update password**.

The link works once, for 24 hours. If it doesn't work, see [a reset or invitation link doesn't work](/troubleshooting/password-reset-token-expired).

## Never signed in before?

You need to accept your invitation first: it's the email titled “Invitation to Join” followed by your company's name. See [sign in for the first time](/getting-started/sign-in). If you can't find it, see [you didn't get an email](/troubleshooting/emails-not-arriving).

## Still stuck?

- **Check the address.** A typo in your email, or a personal address instead of your work one, is the most common cause.
- **Check your keyboard.** Caps Lock or a different keyboard layout changes your password.
- **Ask your HR team.** They can check your email address and role in CentaHR.
- **[Contact support](/contact)** if your HR team can't help.
