## What's missing, and why

| What's missing | Why | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The dashboard | Employees use the employee portal; the dashboard is for HR, admins and line managers. | An HR admin, by changing your role |
| My team | It appears when people report to you. | HR, by setting reporting lines on the org chart |
| Approvals | It appears when you have something to approve. | Nobody: it shows up when there is |
| Settings pages | Settings are for the Super admin, Admin and HR manager roles. | An HR admin |
| Reports | Reports are for the Super admin, Admin and HR manager roles. | An HR admin |
| Calibration | It's for HR, and for managers whose reports manage people too. | HR, through reporting lines |
| Setup checklist | It leaves the menu once every item is done. | Nobody needs to: the settings pages are still there |
| A module, such as Payroll | It depends on your role, or your company doesn't use it. | An HR admin |

## Ask for access

Ask your HR admin. They change roles under their avatar menu → **Users**: they open your row's menu, choose **Edit**, and pick your new role. See [roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

After a role changes, sign out and sign in again to see the new menu.
