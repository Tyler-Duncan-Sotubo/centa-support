The rota shows who works which shift on each day of the week. People with a shift are timed against it; with **Use shifts** on, people without one can't clock in that day.

Go to **Attendance → Rota** and choose the office at the top.

## Give someone a shift

1. Find the person and the day, and click the empty day.
2. In **Add shift**, choose the **Shift** and save.
3. To move a shift to another day, drag it there. To change it, click it and choose **Change shift**.

Use the arrows at the top to plan the weeks ahead.

## Rules the rota follows

- **One shift per person per day.** A second shift on the same day is refused.
- **Shifts match locations.** A person can only be given shifts from the office they're located at.
- **Line managers can plan the rota** for the people below them in the reporting line; HR can plan everyone's.

## When the rota is done

People clock in as usual. Their lateness and overtime are worked out against their shift, and [shift reports](/attendance/use/reports) show who was rostered and who turned up.
