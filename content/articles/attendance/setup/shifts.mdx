A shift is a pattern of hours, such as a morning shift from 7:00 to 15:00. Use shifts when people work different hours; you give people their shifts on the [rota](/attendance/setup/rota).

Shifts only apply when **Use shifts** is on in [attendance settings](/attendance/setup/settings). With it on, people need a shift on the rota for the day to clock in.

## Create a shift

Go to **Attendance → Settings → Shift management** and click **Add shift**.

| | Field | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Office | The office the shift belongs to. Only people located at that office can be given it. |
| 2 | Working days | The days this shift runs. |
| 3 | Late tolerance (minutes) | Minutes after the shift starts before a clock-in counts as late. |
| 4 | Allow early clock-in | Lets people clock in before the shift starts, up to the minutes you set. Off, nobody can clock in before the start time. |
| 5 | Allow late clock-out | Lets people clock out after the shift ends, up to the minutes you set. |

## Things to know

- **Shift times replace the default hours** for anyone on the rota that day. Lateness, early finishes and overtime are worked out against the shift.
- **Changing a shift** affects days from now on. Days already recorded keep their status.
- **Shifts are per office**, so a company with two offices may need a morning shift at each.
