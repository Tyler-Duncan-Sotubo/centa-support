People can clock in within 100 metres of their own location, or of any of your offices. Each office needs its exact position on the map for this to work.

Go to **Staff → Company → Offices**. Your company starts with one office, **Head Office**.

## Add an office

1. Click **Add office**.
2. Under **Details**, enter the **Name**, choose the **Type** and enter the **Address**. City, state, country and postal code are optional.
3. Under **Map position**, place the pin exactly on the building.
4. Save.

## Office types

| Type | Use it for | Who can clock in there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office | A place where your company works | Anyone, whatever their own location |
| Home | Someone's home, for people who work from home | People whose location it is |
| Remote | Another regular place of work, such as a client's site | People whose location it is |

## Give each person their location

Each person's **Location** is on their profile under **Employment details**. They can clock in:

- within 100 metres of their own location, of any type;
- within 100 metres of any office of type Office.

So someone who works from home needs a Home location pinned at their address, and set as their location.

## Let someone clock in at more places

Some people work from two places, such as an office and a client's site. Give them up to two extra locations:

1. On **Staff → Company → Offices**, click **Assign Remote Location**.
2. Choose the **Employee** and the **Remote Location**, and save.

They can then clock in at their own location, at any office, and at up to two extra locations.

## Things to know

- **Pin the building, not the street.** The pin is the centre of the 100-metre circle. A pin placed a block away can block people inside the office.
- **Inactive offices don't count.** People can't clock in at an office you've switched off.
- **Head Office can't be deleted**, but you can rename it and move its pin.
