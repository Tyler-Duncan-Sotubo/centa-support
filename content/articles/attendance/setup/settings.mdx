These settings decide what counts as on time, late and overtime. A new company starts with 9:00 to 17:00, five days a week, and 10 minutes' tolerance for lateness.

Go to **Attendance → Settings → Attendance settings**. Changes save automatically.

| | Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Use shifts | Times people against the shift they're rostered for that day. Without a shift, they can't clock in. Leave it off if everyone works the same hours. | Off |
| 2 | Default start and end time | The working day for anyone without a shift. Clock-ins after the start (plus the late tolerance) are late; clock-outs before the end are early. | 09:00 to 17:00 |
| 3 | Default working days | How many days a week people work. With 5, Saturday and Sunday are the weekend; with 7, there's no weekend. | 5 |
| 4 | Late tolerance | Minutes after the start time before a clock-in counts as late. | 10 |
| 5 | Early clock-in window | How many minutes before the start time people can clock in. | 15 |
| 6 | Block on public holidays | Stops people clocking in on public holidays. | On |
| 7 | Allow overtime | Records time worked after the end time as overtime, paid at the **Overtime rate** you set, for example 1.5 times. | Off |
| 8 | Allow half days | Lets a short day count as a half day, of the **Half-day length** you set. | On, 4 hours |

## Things to know

- **Shifts override these hours.** With **Use shifts** on, each shift's own times and rules apply, and the hours here only cover people without a shift. See [shifts](/attendance/setup/shifts).
- **Times are local.** Each person is timed in the time zone of their office.
- **Changing the hours affects new clock-ins.** Days already recorded keep their status.
