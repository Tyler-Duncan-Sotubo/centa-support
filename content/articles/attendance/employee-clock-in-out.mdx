You clock in when you start work and clock out when you finish, from **Home** in the employee portal or in the Centa HR app. CentaHR checks you're at your workplace each time.

## Clock in

1. Open **Home**. The **Today** panel shows the time and your status: **Not clocked in**.
2. Click **Clock in**.
3. The first time, your browser or phone asks to use your location. Allow it.

You'll see **Clocked in** with the time. The panel then counts the time you've worked so far today.

## Clock out

1. On **Home**, click **Clock out**.
2. Click **Yes, clock out**. You can only clock in once a day, so clock out when you've finished for the day.

## The rules

- **Be at work.** You need to be within 100 metres of your workplace, or of one of your company's offices.
- **Not too early.** You can clock in a few minutes before your start time, but not earlier. Your company sets how many.
- **Once a day.** One clock-in and one clock-out each day.
- **On shifts?** You need a shift on the rota that day.

If something stops you, see [clocking in or out doesn't work](/attendance/not-at-assigned-location).

## Your timesheet

Go to **Timesheet** to see each day of the month: your clock-ins, when you were late, and days absent, on leave or on holiday. If a day is wrong, click **Request a correction** and your line manager will review it. See [correction requests](/attendance/use/corrections).
