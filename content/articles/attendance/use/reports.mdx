Three reports cover attendance over a month. Each has an **Export** button to download it.

Go to **Attendance** and choose a report under **Reports**.

| Report | What it answers | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attendance summary | How attendance is tracking across the company | Days present, late, absent and on leave, and the month for each person |
| Department summary | Which departments attend best and worst | Company attendance, the highest and lowest department, and each department's rate |
| Shift reports | Who was rostered each month, and who turned up | Shifts rostered, people rostered, shift types and turnout; each person's rostered, present and missed shifts. Filter by location and department. |

## Who can see them

Reports are for HR: people with the Super admin, Admin or HR manager role. Line managers see their own people on the [timesheet](/attendance/use/timesheet) instead.

## Using them with payroll

Before you run payroll, check the month's **Attendance summary** for absences and lateness, and make sure any [corrections](/attendance/use/corrections) are decided.
