The timesheet shows one day at a time: who clocked in and out, how long they worked, and each person's status. HR sees everyone; line managers see the people below them in the reporting line.

Go to **Attendance → Timesheet**. Use the arrows beside the date to move between days.

The panel at the top sums up the day. Below it, **By department** compares departments, and the list shows each person. Filter the list by status, or sort it by any column.

## What each status means

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Present | Clocked in on time |
| Late | Clocked in after the start time plus the late tolerance |
| Absent | A working day with no clock-in, no approved leave and no holiday |
| On leave | Approved leave for that day |
| Holiday | A public holiday from **Leave → Settings → Holidays** |
| Weekend | Not a working day, under your **Default working days** |
| Day off | Not rostered to work that day, when you use shifts |

Start times, tolerance and working days come from [attendance settings](/attendance/setup/settings), or from each person's [shift](/attendance/setup/shifts).

## Fixing a day

If someone forgot to clock in or out, or clocked in at the wrong time, they ask for a [correction](/attendance/use/corrections) from their own timesheet. Their line manager approves it, and the timesheet updates.

## For line managers

**My team** shows today at a glance: who's in, late, on leave and not clocked in. Open **Attendance → Timesheet** for the full day, or any past day.
