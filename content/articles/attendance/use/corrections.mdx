A correction fixes a day that's wrong: a forgotten clock-in or clock-out, or the wrong time. The employee asks; their line manager decides; the timesheet changes once it's approved.

## Ask for a correction (employees)

1. Go to **Timesheet** in the employee portal.
2. Click **Request a correction**.
3. Choose the **Day**, then enter the right **Clock-in** time, **Clock-out** time, or both.
4. Under **Why**, say what happened, and click **Send request**.

Requests appear under **Your correction requests** on the same page, with their status.

### Rules for requests

- The day has to be today or in the past.
- One request per day can wait at a time. To change it, wait for a decision first.
- If there's no clock-in that day, the request has to include the time you started.
- The clock-out has to be after the clock-in.

## Approve or reject (line managers)

1. Open **Approvals**. Correction requests show as **Timesheet**.
2. Check the day, the times and the reason.
3. Click **Approve** to update the timesheet, or **Reject**.

Only the person's line manager can decide, or whoever stands in for them. If someone has no line manager, requests go to your company's default manager.

## Things to know

- **Approving changes the day's status.** A corrected clock-in after the start time still counts as late.
- **Rejected requests stay on the employee's list**, so they can see the outcome and send a new one if they need to.
