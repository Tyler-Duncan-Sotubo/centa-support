## Location messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You are not at a valid company location. | You're more than 100 metres from your workplace and from every office. | Move closer and try again. If you're inside the building, ask HR to check the office's pin on the map. |
| Location permission denied. Enable it to clock in. | Your browser or phone is blocking your location. | Allow location for CentaHR in your browser or phone settings, then try again. |
| Geolocation is not supported by this browser. | Your browser can't share your location. | Use an up-to-date browser, or the Centa HR app. |
| No assigned office location for this employee. Please contact HR/admin. | Your profile has no workplace set. | Ask HR to set your **Location**. |
| Assigned office location not found. | Your workplace has been switched off or removed. | Ask HR to check your **Location**. |

## Time and shift messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You are clocking in too early. | It's earlier than your start time allows. | Wait a few minutes, then try again. |
| You cannot clock in before your shift start time. | Your shift doesn't allow clocking in early. | Clock in when your shift starts. |
| You do not have an active shift assigned today. | Your company uses shifts, and you're not on today's rota. | Ask your manager or HR to add you to the rota. |
| You have already clocked in today. | You can clock in once a day. | If the time is wrong, [request a correction](/attendance/use/corrections). |
| You have not clocked in today. | There's no clock-in to clock out from. | Clock in first, or request a correction for the day. |
| You have already clocked out. | You've finished for today. | If the time is wrong, request a correction. |

## Still not working?

- **Turn on precise location.** On phones, approximate location can put you outside the 100 metres.
- **Connect to Wi-Fi.** It usually makes your location more accurate.
- **Try the app** if the browser keeps failing, or the browser if the app does.
- **Fix the day afterwards.** If you couldn't clock in at all, [request a correction](/attendance/use/corrections) so your timesheet shows the right times.

If none of this works, [contact support](/contact) or ask your HR team.
