Clocking in depends on two things: where your offices are, and which office each person works from. Check these before you tell people to start clocking in.

- [ ] **Your offices are added and pinned.** Every office needs its position on the map, or nobody can clock in there. See [offices and clock-in locations](/attendance/setup/offices).
- [ ] **Everyone has a location.** Each person's **Location** is on their profile under **Employment details**. Someone with no location sees “No assigned office location for this employee” when they try to clock in.
- [ ] **People work from home?** Add their home as a location of type Home or Remote, pin it, and set it as their location.
- [ ] **Public holidays are added** under **Leave → Settings → Holidays**, so those days show as holidays, not absences.
- [ ] **Line managers are set.** Correction requests go to each person's line manager. Set reporting lines on **Staff → Company → Org chart**.
- [ ] **People can log in.** Clocking in happens in the employee portal or the Centa HR app.

## Tell your employees

When you're ready, let people know:

1. They clock in and out from **Home** in the employee portal, or from the Centa HR app.
2. Their phone or browser will ask for their location the first time: they need to allow it.
3. They can only clock in once a day, and only near an office.

Share [clock in and out](/attendance/employee-clock-in-out) with them.
