Staff holds your people and the shape of your company. The rest of CentaHR builds on it: payroll pays the salaries recorded here, leave and other requests go to each person's line manager, and clocking in uses each person's office.

## The parts of Staff

| Part | What it is | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Departments and job roles](/staff/company/departments-and-job-roles) | How your company is organised, and the jobs people do | HR |
| [Offices](/attendance/setup/offices) | Where people work, and where they can clock in | HR |
| [Org chart](/staff/company/org-chart) | Who reports to whom | HR, everyone |
| [Teams](/staff/company/teams) | Groups of people that cut across departments | HR |
| [Employees](/staff/people/employees) | Everyone you employ, by status, and the people who've left | HR |

| [Onboarding templates](/staff/lifecycle/onboarding-templates) | What new joiners fill in, and the tasks around their start | HR |
| [Onboard a new joiner](/staff/lifecycle/onboard-a-new-joiner) | Inviting someone and following their progress | HR |
| [Offboarding process](/staff/lifecycle/offboarding-process) | Your reasons for leaving, and your leaving checklist | HR |
| [Offboard someone](/staff/lifecycle/offboard-someone) | Running someone's exit, up to their last day and after | HR, line managers |

Staff is for the Super admin, Admin, HR manager and Recruiter roles. Everyone can see the org chart in the employee portal.

## Set it up

1. Check [before you start](/staff/before-you-start).
2. Create your [job roles and departments](/staff/company/departments-and-job-roles).
3. Add your [offices](/attendance/setup/offices).
4. [Add or import your employees](/staff/people/add-and-import), then give everyone a line manager on the [org chart](/staff/company/org-chart).
5. Set up your [onboarding templates](/staff/lifecycle/onboarding-templates) and your [offboarding process](/staff/lifecycle/offboarding-process).

Each step matches an item on **Staff → Setup checklist**: Create job roles, Set up departments, Add company locations, Upload employees, Create onboarding templates and Define offboarding process.
