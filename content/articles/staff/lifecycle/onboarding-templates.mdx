An onboarding template decides two things: what a new joiner fills in when they first sign in, and the checklist of tasks around their start. You choose a template each time you invite someone, so you can have one for full-time staff and another for interns.

Go to **Staff → Settings → Onboarding templates**.

## Start from a ready-made template

**Templates You Can Clone** has three to start from: **Full-Time Employee Onboarding**, **Intern Onboarding** and **Contract Staff Onboarding**. Clone one, and it appears under **Your Templates** for you to edit.

## Create your own

1. Click **New Template**, and give it a **Template Name** and **Description**.
2. Under **Select Fields to Include**, tick what new joiners should fill in.
3. Drag the fields under **Selected Fields (Drag to Reorder)** into the order they'll see them.
4. Check the **Auto-generated Checklist**, and tick any extra tasks under **Other Task:**.
5. Click **Save Template**.

## What joiners can fill in

| Group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Date of birth, gender, marital status, address, country, phone, and an emergency contact's name and phone |
| Bank and tax | Bank name, account number, account name and branch, TIN, pension PIN and NHF number |
| Documents | A valid government ID |

## The checklist

Tasks come from the fields you pick: personal fields add **Fill personal details**, bank and tax fields add **Add bank and tax info**, and the ID adds **Upload valid government ID**. These are the joiner's, and tick themselves off as they fill them in.

Under **Other Task:**, add tasks for others:

| Task | Who | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign Offer Letter | Employee | 2 days after they start |
| Attend onboarding call | HR | 4 days after they start |
| Share a first-week plan | Manager | 1 day after they start |
| Assign a buddy | Manager | 1 day after they start |
| Hold a 30-day check-in | Manager | 30 days after they start |

## Change a template

Click **Edit** on a template under **Your Templates**, make your changes and save.
