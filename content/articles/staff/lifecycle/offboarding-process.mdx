Set this up once, and every offboarding starts with it. Go to **Staff → Settings → Offboarding process**. **Termination Configuration** has three tabs.

| Tab | What it holds | To start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Termination Types | How someone leaves | Fired, Laid Off, Resigned, Mutual Agreement |
| Termination Reasons | Why they leave | Attendance issues, Misconduct, Rudeness to coworker, Insubordination, Performance |
| Termination Checklist | The tasks to finish before and after they go | Remove employee from Payroll, Remove employee from benefits plan, Calculate vacation payout, Disable email address, Return all company assets |

The ones you start with are marked **Default**. You can't edit or delete them, but you can add your own beside them.

<Callout type="tip" title="Add reasons for people who choose to leave">

The default reasons are all about the person's conduct or work. Add reasons for resignations too, such as Relocation, Further study or A new job.

</Callout>

## Types decide how they leave

Someone whose type mentions resigning or retiring leaves as **Resigned**. Every other type leaves as **Terminated**. Each offboarding shows which, under **Leaves as**.

## Add a checklist task

1. Open **Termination Checklist** and click **Add Item**.
2. Enter the **Name** and an optional **Description**.
3. Tick **The line manager does this** if it's theirs. Otherwise it's HR's.
4. Choose **When it’s due**, from two weeks before the last day to a week after it.
5. Tick **Asset Return Step** for returning equipment. It becomes one task for each asset the person holds.
6. Click **Add**.
