An onboarding invite adds someone with just the essentials. They fill in the rest themselves, following your [onboarding template](/staff/lifecycle/onboarding-templates), when they first sign in.

## Send the invite

1. Go to **Staff → Onboarding** and click **Send onboarding invite**.
2. Under **Template**, choose the **Onboarding template**.
3. Under **Personal**, enter their **Employee ID** (or click **Generate**), **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**.
4. Under **Work**, choose their **Role**, **Department**, **Job Role**, **Cost Center**, **Pay Group** and **Location**, and their **Employment start date**. Missing one? Use **Add new** beside it.
5. Under **Compensation**, enter their yearly **Gross salary (naira)**.
6. Click **Send invite**.

Their status is **Onboarding** until they've finished.

## What they do

They get an email to set a password. When they sign in, they see your onboarding steps, and fill in the fields from your template. See [complete your onboarding](/getting-started/complete-onboarding) for their side.

Once they've finished their steps, they become **Active** and join your [employee list](/staff/people/employees). If they're on probation, set their status and probation end date on their profile.

## Follow their progress

**Staff → Onboarding** lists everyone onboarding, with when they started, their **Progress** and their **Status**: **Pending**, **In Progress** or **Completed**. Filter by status to see who's still going.

Click someone's progress to see their checklist, including the tasks for HR and their manager, such as **Assign a buddy** or **Hold a 30-day check-in**. Managers see their own tasks under **To do for your team**, on **My team**.

## Before their first day

- **Give them a line manager,** so their requests have somewhere to go. See [org chart](/staff/company/org-chart).
- **Check their bank details** once they've added them, before their first pay run.
