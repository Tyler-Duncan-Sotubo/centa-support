Departments and job roles are lists you pick from as you add people, so set them up first. They show on profiles and the org chart, and reports use them, such as payroll costs by department.

## Job roles

A job role is a job people do, such as Account Executive or Operations Manager. Several people can share one.

1. Go to **Staff → Company → Job roles** and click **Add Job Role**.
2. Enter the **Title**, choose the **Level** and add a **Description**.
3. Click **Add**.

The levels are Entry, Junior, Mid, Senior and Lead.

## Departments

1. Go to **Staff → Company → Departments** and click **Add Department**.
2. Enter the **Name**, and choose the **Head of Department** if you've added them already.
3. Click **Add**.

People join a department when you add them, or later from the **Employment** tab of their profile.

## Add a lot at once

Both pages have **Import**:

1. Click **Import**, then **Download Example** for the spreadsheet layout.
2. Fill it in, one department or job role per row.
3. Drag the file into the window, or click to choose it, then click **Upload**.

## Change or remove one

Use the menu on a row to **Edit** or **Delete** it. Before deleting, move anyone in that department or job role to another one.
