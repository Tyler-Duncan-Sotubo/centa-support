Departments follow your structure. Teams are for anything else: a project, a squad that spans departments, or an interest group. Performance uses them for team objectives, and Benefits for who a plan covers.

Go to **Staff → Teams**.

## Create a team

1. Click **Create Group**.
2. Enter the **Group Name**, for example Engineering Team. The **Slug** fills itself in from the name.
3. Choose the **Group Type**.
4. Add people under **Team members**, then choose the team's **Lead** from them.
5. Click **Create**.

The **Group Type** is TEAM, PROJECT, INTEREST or SECURITY. It describes the team, and you can filter the list by it.

## Manage teams

- **Search** by team or lead, and filter by type.
- To add or remove people, edit the team and change its **Team members**.
- Deleting a team removes it and its memberships, and can't be undone.

A team lead doesn't become anyone's line manager. Reporting lines come from the [org chart](/staff/company/org-chart).
