The org chart is built from each person's line manager. It matters beyond the picture: a person's line manager approves their leave and other requests, sees their records, and runs their reviews.

Go to **Staff → Company → Org chart**. The heading shows how many people, departments and levels you have.

## Find your way around

- **Search** by name, job title or department, and the chart moves to them.
- **Highlight a department** to pick out its people.
- **Chart** or **List** switches between the picture and an indented list. **Expand all** and **Collapse** open and close every branch.
- **Find me** jumps to you.

Click anyone to see who they report to and their direct reports. **View profile** opens their record.

## Give someone a line manager

1. Click the person on the chart.
2. Under **Reports to**, click **Change manager** <Mark n={1} />.
3. Choose their manager under **Choose a manager**, and click **Save**.

Someone with no manager sits at the top of the chart, marked **No manager: top of the organisation.** Only your most senior person should be there. You can also change someone's manager from the **Employment** tab of their profile, under **Reporting Manager**.

<Callout type="note" title="People without a line manager">

Their requests go to your default manager instead, so choose one. See [roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

</Callout>

## Fix reporting lines

If anything is wrong, a warning appears above the chart: **Some reporting lines need fixing**.

- **Someone reports to a person who's left.** They show at the top until they have a new manager. Click their name and give them one.
- **A reporting loop,** where two people end up reporting to each other. Change one of their managers to break it.

When someone is leaving, you can move their direct reports to a new manager as part of their offboarding. See [offboard someone](/staff/lifecycle/offboard-someone).
