## The tabs

| Tab | What's on it |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Their personal information, education and employment history, family and dependents, and certifications |
| Employment | Their job, office, pay group, status and dates; their reporting manager; their salary; and their bank and tax details |
| Payroll | Their payslips, month by month, to download |
| Leave | Their leave balances and requests |
| Attendance | Their clock-ins and hours |
| Documents | Their documents, such as contracts and IDs |

## Change their details

Each card has an edit button. Use it to update a card and save.

- **A new job or department:** edit **Employment Details**. You can also change their status, start date, whether they're confirmed and their **Role** in CentaHR here.
- **A pay rise:** edit **Compensation** with the new yearly gross salary and the date it takes effect. The next pay run uses it.
- **New bank details:** edit **Financials**. Check them before the next pay run.
- **A new manager:** click **Change Manager** under **Reporting Manager**, choose them, and save.
- **Family, history and certifications:** add or edit entries on the **Personal** tab.

Employees can update much of their own personal information from their profile in the employee portal. See [your profile](/getting-started/your-profile).

## When someone leaves

Click **Terminate** on the **Employment** tab to start their offboarding. While it's under way, the button becomes **View offboarding**. See [offboard someone](/staff/lifecycle/offboard-someone).
