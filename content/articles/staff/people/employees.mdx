Search by name, email or employee ID. Click someone, or **View profile** in their menu, to open their [profile](/staff/people/employee-profile).

## Statuses

**All** lists everyone still employed. Then there's a tab for each status in use, and **Former** for people who've left.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Working and confirmed |
| Probation | Working, but not confirmed yet. Their probation end date is on their profile. |
| On Leave | Away for a long stretch, such as maternity leave |
| Resigned, Terminated, Inactive | Left the company. They're under **Former**, and can no longer sign in. |

People you've invited through onboarding join this list once they've completed their onboarding steps. Until then, follow them on [Staff → Onboarding](/staff/lifecycle/onboard-a-new-joiner).

## Probation

People on probation have a **Probation End Date**. Two weeks and three days before it, their line manager is asked what should happen.

1. The manager opens **My team**, finds the person under what's coming up, and clicks **Recommend**.
2. They choose **Confirm them**, **Extend probation** with a new end date, or **End employment**, add why if they like, and click **Send to HR**.
3. HR approves or declines it under **Approvals**. Approving a confirmation makes the person **Active**. Approving an extension moves their end date. To end someone's employment, HR then [offboards them](/staff/lifecycle/offboard-someone).

## Former employees

**Former** lists people who've left, from the day after their last day. Their records stay, so you can still look them up.

## Export the list

Click **Export** and choose **Current employees**, **Former employees** or **Everyone**. You get a spreadsheet with each person's employee number, name, email, department, job role, location, pay group, cost center, status, dates, role and manager.

Salaries and bank details are left out, so the export is safe to share for headcount and planning.
