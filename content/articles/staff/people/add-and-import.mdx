Add the people who already work for you here. For someone about to start, [send an onboarding invite](/staff/lifecycle/onboard-a-new-joiner) instead, so they fill in their own details.

However you add someone, they get an email inviting them to set a password and sign in.

## Add one person

On **Staff → Employees**, click **Add employee**. **Invite Employees** has five parts:

| Part | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Basic Info | Employee ID, first and last name, and work email |
| Work | Department, job role, location and pay group; employment status, start date, probation end date and whether they're confirmed; and their **Role**, which decides what they can do in CentaHR |
| Personal | Date of birth, phone, gender, marital status, address, state, country and an emergency contact |
| Financial & Compensation Info | Yearly gross salary, TIN, pension PIN, NHF number, bank and account number, and whether NHF applies |
| Dependents | Family members, with their relationship and date of birth, and whether each is a beneficiary |

Click **Submit**.

- **The bank account is checked** as you enter it. **Account Verified** shows the name on the account, so you can see it's the right one.
- **Gross salary is yearly.** Payroll pays a twelfth of it each month.
- **Most people are Team Members.** See [roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access) for the others.

Then give them a line manager: on their profile's **Employment** tab, click **Change Manager** under **Reporting Manager**. Or use the [org chart](/staff/company/org-chart).

## Import many people

1. On **Staff → Employees**, click **Upload employees**.
2. Click **Download Example**, and fill in the spreadsheet with one person per row.
3. Drag the file into **Upload CSV File**, or click to choose it. CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files both work.
4. Click **Add**.

Each person gets their invitation. Rows with a problem are skipped and the rest are added, and you're told which rows to fix and upload again.

### Getting the file right

- **Employee Number and Email are required** for every row. Each email can only belong to one person.
- **Names must match CentaHR exactly:** department, job role, location and pay group. Create any that are missing first. See [departments and job roles](/staff/company/departments-and-job-roles).
- **Manager Email** sets each person's line manager. It can be someone else in the same file.
- **Have a Managing Director job role.** People with no manager email report to whoever holds it. The import won't run without it.
- **Is Head of Department** is Yes or No, with one head per department.
