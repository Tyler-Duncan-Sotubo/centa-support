Adding people goes quickly when you have everything to hand. Gather these first.

## Your company

- [ ] **Your departments,** and who heads each one.
- [ ] **Your job titles,** each with a level: Entry, Junior, Mid, Senior or Lead.
- [ ] **Your offices,** with their addresses. You'll pin each one on a map.

## Your people

For each person:

- [ ] **Name, work email and employee ID.** Their invitation goes to that email, and they sign in with it.
- [ ] **Their job:** job role, department, office, start date and employment status, and when their probation ends if they're on probation.
- [ ] **Their line manager.** Leave, reimbursements and other requests go to them.
- [ ] **Personal details:** date of birth, phone, gender, marital status, address and an emergency contact. Gender matters for leave only women or only men can take.
- [ ] **Pay details:** yearly gross salary, bank name and account number, tax identification number (TIN), pension PIN and, if it applies, NHF number.
- [ ] **What they can access.** Most people are Team Members. Decide who needs more, such as an HR manager. See [roles and access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

New joiners can fill in their own personal and bank details if you [send them an onboarding invite](/staff/lifecycle/onboard-a-new-joiner) instead.

<Callout type="tip" title="A lot of people to add?">

Use the example spreadsheet in the upload window as your template, and fill it in from your current records. See [add and import employees](/staff/people/add-and-import).

</Callout>

## Joiners and leavers

- [ ] **What new joiners fill in,** such as bank details or a government ID, and the tasks for HR and managers in their first weeks.
- [ ] **What happens when someone leaves:** the tasks to finish, such as getting equipment back or taking them off payroll, and who does each one.
