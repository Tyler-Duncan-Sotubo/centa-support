- **This pay period** shows gross pay, net pay, deductions and bonuses. Choose another month at the top of the card.
- **Year to date** adds up the same figures for this year.
- **Payroll by month** charts net pay and deductions over the last 12 months. It appears after your first pay run.
- **At a glance** shows how many employees you pay, your monthly salaries and your tax filing date.

## Reports

Go to **Payroll → Reports** and pick one.

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Payroll history | Every pay run, with its cost and status |
| Salary report | Pay by employee, salary bands and spend by department |
| Deductions | What was deducted from pay, by month and by employee |
| Year to date | Payroll totals for the current year |
| Off-cycle payroll | Payments made outside the regular pay run |
| Payroll variance | How the latest pay run differs from the one before |
| Payroll cost | Gross pay by department and pay group |
| GL summary | Payroll journal lines for your general ledger |
| Loan monthly summary | Salary advances issued, repaid and still outstanding |
| Loan repayments | What each employee has repaid so far |
| Reimbursements | All reimbursement records |

## Each month

- **Before approving,** check **Payroll variance** for anything that changed from last month.
- **After paying,** send the **GL summary** to your accountants, and pay what's due under [remittances](/payroll/use/remittances).
- **At the end of the year,** **Year to date** gives each person's totals.
