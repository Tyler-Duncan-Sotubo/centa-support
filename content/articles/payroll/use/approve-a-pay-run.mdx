When a pay run is sent for approval, your approvers approve it one at a time, in the order set in [general settings](/payroll/setup/settings). When it's a step's turn, everyone with that step's role gets an email, **Action Required: Approve Payroll for** the month. Any one of them can approve.

## Approve

1. Click **Review & Approve** in the email. It opens **Payroll approval** for that month.
2. Click **Detailed report** to download everyone's pay, and check it.
3. Under **Your approval**, add any remarks, and click **Approve**.

- **Workflow** lists each approver role, with whether it has approved.
- **Your approval** only lets you approve when it's your turn. Otherwise it says who it's waiting for, or **Every approval is complete.** once everyone has approved.
- **Approved so far** lists the roles that have approved.

Once the last approver approves, the run is approved. Payslips are created, and whoever is running payroll can download the bank advice and pay people. See [run payroll](/payroll/use/run-payroll).

## Before you approve

- **Compare with last month.** The [Payroll variance](/payroll/use/reports) report shows how this run differs from the one before. Ask about anything unexpected.
- **Check joiners and leavers** have the right pay for the days they worked.
- **Something wrong?** Don't approve. Tell whoever is running payroll: they can fix it, sync the run and send it again.
