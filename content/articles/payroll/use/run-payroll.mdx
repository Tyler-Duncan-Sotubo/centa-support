A pay run has four steps, shown across the top of the page: **Start**, **Review**, **Approve** and **Pay**. Go to **Payroll → Run payroll**.

## 1. Start

Pick the pay date and click **Calculate payroll**. CentaHR works out everyone's pay for that month.

## 2. Review

Check the figures before anything goes for approval.

- **Totals** at the top: employees, gross pay, deductions and net pay.
- **Each person's pay:** basic, housing, transport, PAYE, pension, NHF, additional deductions, bonus, monthly gross and net salary.
- **Changed something?** Click **Sync payroll** to recalculate. It also brings in people who left this month, so their final pay is included.
- **Started wrong?** **Discard** removes the run's calculations so you can start again.

When it's right, click **Continue to approval**.

## 3. Approve

Click **Send for approval**. Your approvers are told, and the page waits; it moves on by itself once the run is approved. If you don't use an approval workflow, the run is approved automatically. See [approve a pay run](/payroll/use/approve-a-pay-run).

## 4. Pay

1. Check the payment summary: total payroll cost, employee net pay, and the change from last payroll.
2. Click **Download bank advice** and pay people from your bank. **Open the detailed report** gives the full breakdown.
3. Click **Finish pay run**.

The run is done. Payslips are ready on the page, and each employee can see theirs. The remittances due appear under [remittances](/payroll/use/remittances).

## Things to know

- **Leave and attendance can affect pay.** Unpaid leave and absences can reduce pay for the month, so check [leave](/leave/use/requests) and [attendance](/attendance/use/timesheet) are up to date before you calculate.
- **Salary advances** are repaid automatically from each payslip until they're cleared. See [salary advances](/payroll/use/salary-advances).
