Each pay run creates the payments you owe on behalf of your employees: PAYE to the tax authority, pension to each person's pension administrator, NHF, and any other deductions you pass on, such as a cooperative. **Remittances** lists them, so nothing is missed.

Go to **Payroll → Remittances**.

**To remit** is the total of everything still pending. Switch between **Pending** and **Completed** above the list.

## Pay a remittance

1. Open a remittance's menu and choose **View details**. You see its status, the **Amount due** and the **Deposit period**.
2. Click **Download** for the filing as a spreadsheet, with each employee's share. Pension filings include each person's pension PIN and the employer's contribution.
3. Pay the authority or administrator, using the spreadsheet for the schedule they ask for.
4. Click **Mark as paid**. The remittance moves to **Completed**.

Your company's tax and pension details (TIN, pension and NHF numbers) come from [general settings](/payroll/setup/settings).

<Callout type="note" title="Deadlines">

Each authority sets its own deadline, usually early in the month after payroll. Check with your tax adviser for the deadlines that apply to you.
