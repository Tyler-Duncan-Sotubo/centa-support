A salary advance pays part of someone's salary early. They repay it in monthly amounts taken from their payslips until it's cleared.

Go to **Payroll → Salary advance**.

- **Ongoing** has requests waiting for review and advances being repaid. **Settled** has the ones fully repaid.
- **History** lists every action on an advance, with the reason, who took it and when.
- **Outstanding** and **Ongoing advances** count approved advances still being repaid. **Repaid to date** includes settled ones.

## 1. Turn them on and set the limits

Open the **Settings** tab and switch on **Salary advance settings**. Employees can then ask for one. Set the **Minimum Advance Amount (₦)** and **Maximum Advance Amount (₦)**: they start at ₦10,000 and ₦1,000,000.

## 2. Review a request

1. Open the request's menu and choose **Edit**. **Review loan request** opens.
2. Under **Review salary advance**, choose **Approved** or **Rejected**. If you reject it, add a **Reason if rejected**.
3. Save. The employee is told.

An approved advance is repaid from each payslip, by the monthly amount agreed, until nothing is outstanding. It then shows as **Paid** and moves to **Settled**.

## Add one for someone

Click **Add request**. Under **Add salary advance**, enter its **Name**, **Amount**, **Length (Months)** and **Preferred monthly payment**, choose who it's for under **Assign to**, and click **Add**.

## The rules

- **Within your limits.** Advances are between your minimum and maximum, and no more than half the person's yearly gross salary.
- **One at a time.** Someone repaying an advance can't take another until it's cleared. A rejected request doesn't count.
- **The sums add up.** The monthly payment times the number of months equals the amount.
