Payroll is only as right as the details behind it. Check these for every employee, and for your company, before your first pay run.

## For each employee

On their profile, check **Compensation** and their financial details:

- [ ] **Gross salary.** Enter the **yearly** gross. CentaHR pays a twelfth of it each month.
- [ ] **Annual rent,** for anyone who pays rent. PAYE rent relief is 20% of it, up to ₦500,000, and without it there's no rent relief. Add it under **Compensation** on their profile.
- [ ] **Bank name, account number and account name,** for the bank advice.
- [ ] **Tax identification number** and **pension PIN**, for PAYE and pension remittances.
- [ ] **NHF number,** and whether NHF applies to them.
- [ ] **Start date,** so someone who joins mid-month is paid for the days they worked, if you prorate.
- [ ] **Status.** Everyone active, on probation or on leave is paid. People who've left aren't, unless you choose to include leavers in a run.

Salary changes have an **Effective** date on the Compensation card. Pay runs use the salary in effect on the pay date.

## For your company

- [ ] **Company TIN and VAT number,** asked for when you switch on PAYE in [general settings](/payroll/setup/settings).
- [ ] **Pension and NHF numbers,** asked for when you switch those on.
- [ ] **Your logo and company details** under your avatar menu → **Company**. They appear on payslips.

## Moving from another payroll system?

Run your first CentaHR payroll alongside your old one, and compare each person's net pay before you pay from CentaHR. If you need year-to-date figures brought over, [contact support](/contact).
