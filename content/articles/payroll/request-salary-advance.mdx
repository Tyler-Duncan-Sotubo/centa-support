A salary advance pays you part of your salary early. You pay it back in equal monthly amounts taken from your payslips.

## Ask for an advance

1. Go to **Salary advance**, under **Pay and perks** in the employee portal, and click **New request**. Or use **Salary advance** on **Home**.
2. Give it a **Name**, such as School Fees.
3. Enter the **Amount**, the **Tenure (months)** to pay it back over, and the **Monthly payment**.
4. Click **Submit request**.

Your HR or payroll team reviews it, and you're told when it's approved or rejected.

## Follow your repayments

**Salary advance** shows the advance you're repaying: how much you've **Repaid**, what's **Remaining**, your **Monthly** amount and the date of your **Next payment**. Advances you've paid off are listed below it.

Each month's repayment is taken from your payslip, where it shows as **Salary Advance**, until it's cleared.

## If your request isn't accepted

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly payment × tenure must equal total amount | Change the amount, the months or the monthly payment so they multiply out |
| Requested amount is below the minimum allowed | Ask for at least the minimum shown |
| Requested amount exceeds the maximum allowed | Ask for no more than the maximum shown |
| Requested amount exceeds max allowed based on salary | Ask for less. Advances are usually capped at half your yearly salary. |
| The employee already has an active loan | Finish repaying your current advance first |
| Salary advance is not enabled for this company | Your company doesn't offer salary advances. Ask HR. |
