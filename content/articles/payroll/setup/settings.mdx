## Statutory deductions

Switch on each one your company pays. Switching one on asks for its details: your **Company TIN** and **VAT number** for PAYE, your **Pension number** for pension, and your **NHF number** for NHF.

| Deduction | What CentaHR does |
| --- | --- |
| PAYE | Works out income tax on each person's yearly pay, after pension, NHF and rent relief, using the PAYE bands, and takes a twelfth each month. Rent relief is 20% of the **Annual rent** on their profile, up to ₦500,000. |
| Pension | Takes 8% from the employee and adds 10% from the employer, on basic, housing and transport, to start with |
| NHF | Takes 2.5% of basic pay for the National Housing Fund, for people it applies to |
| NHIS | National Health Insurance Scheme contributions |
| NSITF | The employer's Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund contribution |

A new company starts with only NSITF switched on: switch on the rest before your first pay run.

## Salary structure

Gross pay is split into parts that must add up to 100%. To start with: Basic 50%, Housing 25%, Transport 10%, and Utility 3%, Medical 3%, Meal 2%, Data 1% and Balance 6%. Change the percentages, remove parts, or add your own under **Add an allowance**.

The split matters: pension and NHF are worked out on parts of pay, and payslips show each part.

## Approval workflow

With **Multi-level approval** off, a pay run is approved automatically as soon as it's calculated, and payslips are created then. Check the figures before you calculate. Switch it on to need approval, and tick the **Approvers**: Super admin, Finance officer, Payroll specialist or HR manager. They approve in turn. **Fallback approvers** step in when an approver isn't available.

## Proration

Switch on **Proration** to pay part of a salary when someone joins or leaves mid-month. Choose how days are counted under **Calculate using**: **Calendar days**, or **Working days** (Monday to Friday).

## 13th month salary

Switch it on to pay an extra month's salary once a year. Choose the **Payment date** and **Payment type**: a **Fixed amount** in naira, or a **% of gross salary**.
