Pay adjustments are amounts added to or taken from one person's pay, beyond salary and statutory deductions: a cooperative contribution, a staff loan repayment, a sales bonus.

Go to **Payroll → Settings → Pay adjustments**. The page has two tabs: **Deductions** and **Bonuses**.

## Deductions

Click **Add deduction**.

1. Choose the **Deduction type**.
2. Choose the **Rate type**: **Fixed** for an amount, or **Percentage** of pay.
3. Enter the **Rate value**.
4. Choose the **Start date** and **End date**: the deduction applies to every pay run between them.
5. Choose who it's for under **Assign to**, and save.

Deleting a deduction stops it from the next pay run.

## Bonuses

On the **Bonuses** tab, click **Add bonus**. Choose the **Bonus type**, enter the **Amount**, choose who it's for under **Assign to**, and save. It's paid in the next pay run.

## One-off changes in a pay run

For a change to a single month, you can also add bonuses or deductions while reviewing a pay run. See [run payroll](/payroll/use/run-payroll).
