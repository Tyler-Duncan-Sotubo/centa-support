A pay schedule sets how often and when people are paid. A pay group puts together people who share a schedule and the same statutory deductions. Cost centers let you track where payroll costs go.

## Pay schedules

Go to **Payroll → Settings → Pay schedules** and create one.

1. Choose the **Pay frequency**: **Monthly**, or **Biweekly** (every other week).
2. Choose the **Start date**: the first pay date. Later pay dates follow from it.
3. Choose the **Holiday adjustment**: when a pay date falls on a public holiday, people are paid on the **Previous Business Day**. Pay dates on a weekend move to the Friday before.

Most Nigerian companies pay monthly, around the 28th.

## Pay groups

Go to **Payroll → Settings → Pay groups** and click **Add pay group**.

1. Enter the **Group name**, for example Lagos staff or Contract staff.
2. Choose which deductions apply: **Apply PAYE**, **Apply Pension** and **Apply NHF**.
3. Choose the group's pay schedule under **Select pay schedule**.
4. Add the people in the group, and save.

Use groups when some people are paid differently, such as contractors who don't have pension deducted.

## Cost centers

Go to **Payroll → Settings → Cost centers** and click **Add cost center**. Enter its **Name**, a **Code** and a **Budget**. To add many, click **Import**. Payroll cost reports then show spending by cost center.
