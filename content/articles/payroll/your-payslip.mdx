Your payslips are on your profile. Each month's payslip is added once that month's payroll is approved.

1. Go to **Profile** in the employee portal, and open the **Payroll** tab.
2. **Payslip Summary** lists each month. Choose a year to see older ones.
3. Click **PDF** beside a month to download that payslip.

## What's on your payslip

Your net pay is at the top. Below it, each line shows this month's amount and your total so far this year (YTD).

| Line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Basic, Housing, Transport | Your pay, split the way your company sets it |
| Other Allowance | The rest of your gross pay, such as utility or meal allowances |
| Gross Earnings | Your pay for the month before anything is taken off: usually a twelfth of your yearly salary |
| PAYE Tax | Income tax, paid to the tax authority for you |
| Pension Contribution | Usually 8% of your basic, housing and transport, paid to your pension administrator. Your employer adds 10% on top, which isn't taken from your pay. |
| NHF Contribution | National Housing Fund, 2.5% of your basic pay, if it applies to you |
| Other deductions | Each one by name, such as a cooperative contribution |
| Salary Advance | This month's repayment, if you're repaying one |
| Bonus | Any bonus paid this month, on top of your gross earnings |
| Other Payments | Anything else paid with your salary, such as an allowance that isn't taxed |
| Reimbursements | Approved reimbursements, paid back to you with your salary |
| Net pay | Gross earnings, plus any bonus and other payments, less total deductions, plus reimbursements. It matches the net pay at the top, and it's what's paid into your bank account. |

For example, take a gross of ₦500,000: ₦250,000 basic, ₦125,000 housing, ₦50,000 transport and ₦75,000 other. Pension is ₦34,000 and NHF is ₦6,250. For someone paying ₦1,000,000 a year in rent, PAYE is ₦62,255. That leaves ₦397,495.

## Something looks wrong?

- **Joined or left mid-month?** You may be paid only for the days you worked.
- **Unpaid leave** can reduce your pay for the month.
- **Still unsure?** Ask your HR or payroll team. They can see how your pay was worked out.
