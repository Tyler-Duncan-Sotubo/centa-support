You request leave from the employee portal or the Centa HR app. Your line manager decides, and you're told when they do.

## Check your balance

Go to **Leave**. Your balances show for each kind of leave, with your requests below. Filter them by **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**.

If you see **No leave balance yet**, your company hasn't set your leave up yet. Ask your HR team.

## Ask for leave

1. Go to **Leave** and click **New request**, or use **Request leave** on **Home**.
2. Choose the **Leave type**, **Start date** and **End date**.
3. Add a **Reason or notes**, if you like.
4. Check **Working days requested** and **Balance left**, then click **Submit request**.

Weekends and public holidays aren't counted, so a week off is usually five working days.

## After you ask

- **Pending:** waiting for your line manager, or for each person in turn if your company uses more than one approver.
- **Approved:** it's booked. It shows on your timesheet and your team's calendar.
- **Rejected:** your manager may add a reason. You can ask for different dates.

If your request is refused straight away, CentaHR says why. See [why a leave request is refused](/leave/request-problems).
