**Leave management** is where HR and line managers see every request, decide the ones waiting, and check balances. HR sees everyone; line managers see the people below them.

Go to **Leave → Leave management**.

## Decide a request

1. On the **Requests** tab, filter to **Pending**.
2. Open a request's menu and choose **Review**.
3. Check the employee, type, dates, days and reason.
4. Choose a **Decision**, **Approve** or **Reject**, add a reason if you like, and click **Save decision**.

Line managers can also decide requests from **Approvals**. With a multi-level chain, each step decides in turn, and the request is approved once the last step approves.

## Requests that wait too long

With **Auto-approve after** set in [leave settings](/leave/setup/settings), a request nobody has decided approves itself after that many days. Leave that has already started is left for a person to decide. Approvers can also get a reminder, and requests can escalate to their manager and HR, under your avatar menu → **Users** → **Approvals** → **Waiting approvals**.

## Check balances

The **Balances** tab lists everyone with their department, job role and **Days left**. For a breakdown by leave type and year, use the [leave balance report](/leave/use/calendar-and-reports).

## Add leave for someone

For someone who can't request it themselves, such as sick leave phoned in:

1. Click **Request leave**.
2. Choose the **Employee**, **Leave type**, **Start date** and **End date**.
3. For half a day, choose AM or PM under **Partial day (optional)**.
4. Add a **Reason**, and click **Submit request**.
