The calendar shows approved leave and upcoming public holidays, so you can see who's off before you plan. The reports show balances and where leave is going.

## The leave calendar

Go to **Leave → Calendar**. Switch between **Month** and **Timeline**, and use the arrows to move between months.

Each leave type shows in its **Color tag** from [leave types](/leave/setup/types-and-policies). Line managers also see their team's leave on **My team**.

## Reports

Go to **Leave** and choose a report under **Reports**.

| Report | What it answers | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leave balance | How much leave everyone has left | Entitlement, used and remaining days for every employee. Filter by year and leave type. |
| Leave utilization | Where leave days are going | Days requested to date, by leave type and by department |

Use **Leave balance** at the end of the year to check carryover, and before payroll to check unpaid leave.
