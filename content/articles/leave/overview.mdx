Leave covers everything from annual leave to sick days. Employees request leave from the portal or the app; CentaHR checks the request against your rules and their balance, sends it for approval, and updates their balance and the calendar once it's decided.

## Set it up

1. Check [before you start](/leave/before-you-start).
2. Choose your [leave settings](/leave/setup/settings).
3. Create your [leave types and policies](/leave/setup/types-and-policies). Nobody can request leave until a type has a policy.
4. Add your [public holidays](/leave/setup/holidays).
5. Add any [blocked or reserved days](/leave/setup/blocked-and-reserved-days).

Each step matches an item on **Leave → Setup checklist**: Leave settings, Leave types and policies, Holidays, Blocked days and Reserved days.
