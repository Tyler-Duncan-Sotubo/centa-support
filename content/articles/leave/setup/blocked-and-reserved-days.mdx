Blocked days are dates nobody can take leave, such as a stock count or an audit. Reserved days are days set aside for one person, for a particular kind of leave. Requests that include either are refused, with the date that clashed.

## Blocked days

Go to **Leave → Settings → Blocked days** and click **Add Blocked Day**.

1. Enter a **Name**, for example Year-end stock count.
2. Choose the **Date**.
3. Add a **Reason**, so people know why, and save.

Anyone who asks for leave on that date sees “Cannot request leave on blocked day”, with the date.

## Reserved days

Go to **Leave → Settings → Reserved days** and click **Add Reserved Day**.

1. Choose the **Employee**.
2. Choose the **Start date** and **End date**.
3. Choose the **Leave type** the days are for, add a **Reason**, and save.

Use reserved days to hold dates you've already agreed with someone, such as a planned team retreat, so other leave requests can't take them.

## Things to know

- **Requests are checked when they're made.** Adding a blocked day doesn't cancel leave that's already approved for that date; review those requests in [leave management](/leave/use/requests).
- **Tell people early.** Blocked days work best when everyone knows about them before they plan their leave.
