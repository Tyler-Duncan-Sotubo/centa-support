A leave type is a kind of leave, such as Annual Leave or Sick Leave. A policy says how much of it people get, how they earn it, and who can take it. People can only request a type that has a policy.

Go to **Leave → Settings → Leave types & policies**. The page has two tabs: **Types** and **Policies**.

## 1. Create your leave types

1. On the **Types** tab, click **Add leave type**.
2. Enter the **Name**, for example Sick Leave.
3. Choose whether it's paid under **Is Paid?**, and pick a **Color tag** for the calendar.
4. Save.

Most companies start with Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave, Compassionate Leave and Unpaid Leave. To add many at once, click **Import**.

## 2. Give each type a policy

On the **Policies** tab, click **Add policy**.

| | Field | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Build up over time | On, people earn the leave gradually. Off, they get the whole allowance at the start of the year. |
| 2 | Allow carryover | Unused days roll into next year, up to the carryover limit in [leave settings](/leave/setup/settings). |
| 3 | Can be split | On, the leave can be taken in separate blocks. Off, it has to be taken in one continuous period. |
| 4 | Frequency and Days earned each time | How often leave builds up (Monthly, Quarterly or Annually) and how much each time. 1.67 days a month is about 20 days a year. With building up off, **Days earned each time** is the yearly allowance. |
| 5 | Maximum balance (days) | The most anyone can hold. Building up stops at this number. |
| 6 | Who can take it | **Everyone**, **Women only** or **Men only**, for leave such as maternity or paternity leave. |

## Examples

| Leave type | A typical policy |
| --- | --- |
| Annual Leave | Builds up monthly, 1.67 days each time, carryover allowed, can be split |
| Sick Leave | Doesn't build up: 10 days at the start of the year, can be split |
| Maternity Leave | Doesn't build up: the full allowance, women only, can't be split |
| Unpaid Leave | Doesn't build up, with the days your company allows |

## Things to know

- **Balances appear overnight.** After you create a policy, each eligible person's balance for the year is set up overnight; leave that builds up monthly is added on the first of each month.
- **Unconfirmed employees** only get the types allowed in [leave settings](/leave/setup/settings) until they're confirmed.
