These settings apply to every kind of leave. A new company starts with the line manager approving, 20 days a year, up to 5 days carried over, and weekends and public holidays left out of leave days.

Go to **Leave → Settings → Leave settings**. Changes save as you make them.

## Approval workflow

With **Multi-level approval** off, each person's line manager approves their leave. Switch it on to add steps; each step decides in order.

- **Who can approve a step:** from the reporting line (**Line manager**, **Manager's manager** or **Department head**), anyone with a role such as HR manager, or **A specific person**.
- **Nobody approves their own leave.** A step that would land on the person asking is skipped, so an HR manager's own request goes to another HR manager. A step that finds nobody, such as **Line manager** for someone without one, is skipped too. A line manager who has left is passed over for the manager above them.
- **HR covers other roles.** A step for a role such as Finance that finds nobody (no one has the role, they've left, or the only one is the person asking) goes to an HR manager instead of being skipped. If its only holder is on leave with no stand-in, HR can approve while they're away.
- **When no step finds anyone,** an HR manager approves, then an admin. The super admin, usually the CEO, approves only when there's nobody else.
- **Only when over:** later steps can apply only to longer requests, for example HR only for requests over 5 days.
- **Auto-approve after** (1): requests with no decision after this many days approve themselves. Leave that has already started is left for a person to decide. Set 0 to turn it off.

## Entitlement

| | Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Annual leave entitlement | The yearly allowance of annual leave | 20 days |
| 3 | Allow carryover of unused leave | Lets unused days roll into next year, up to the **Carryover limit** | On, 5 days |
| 4 | Allow negative leave balance | Lets people take more leave than they have, going below zero | Off |
| 5 | Minimum notice and Maximum consecutive days | How many days ahead a request must be made, and the most days one request can cover. Requests outside these are refused with the reason. | 3 days, 30 days |

## Eligibility and days

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Allow leave for unconfirmed employees | Lets people still on probation request any leave. Off, they can only request the **Allowed leave types**. | Off: Sick Leave and Unpaid Leave only |
| Exclude weekends from leave days | Leaves the **Weekend days** out when counting a request | On: Saturday and Sunday |
| Exclude public holidays | Leaves your [holidays](/leave/setup/holidays) out when counting a request | On |

## Notifications

Choose who hears about requests: **Notify HR**, **Notify approver**, **Notify line manager**, and **Notify employee when approved or rejected**. To start with, the approver hears about new requests and the employee hears the decision, by email. **CC roles** copies people with the roles you list.
