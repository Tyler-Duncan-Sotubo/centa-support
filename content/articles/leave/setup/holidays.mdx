Holidays are days nobody is expected to work. They're left out when CentaHR counts leave days, they show on the leave calendar, and the attendance timesheet marks them as holidays instead of absences.

Go to **Leave → Settings → Holidays**.

## Add a holiday

1. Click **Add Holiday**.
2. Enter the **Holiday name**, **Date** and **Year**.
3. Enter a **Type**, such as Public or Company, and choose the **Country**.
4. Save.

To add a year's holidays at once, click **Import**.

## Things to know

- **Add next year's holidays early.** People request leave months ahead, and a missing holiday would be counted as a leave day.
- **Movable holidays,** such as Eid, are announced late. Add them as soon as the date is confirmed.
- **Company days off,** such as a founders' day, can be added the same way with a Type of Company.
- **Stopping people working on holidays** is set in Attendance: see **Block on public holidays** in [attendance settings](/attendance/setup/settings).
