Leave relies on three things from your people data: who approves for whom, who's confirmed, and who's a man or a woman for leave that only applies to one. Check these first.

- [ ] **Everyone has a line manager.** Leave requests go to the line manager first. Set reporting lines on **Staff → Company → Org chart**, and choose a default manager for anyone without one under your avatar menu → **Users** → **Approvals**.
- [ ] **Confirmations are up to date.** People still on probation are unconfirmed, and can only request the leave types you allow for them (sick and unpaid leave, to start with).
- [ ] **Gender is filled in** on profiles, if you'll have leave only women or only men can take, such as maternity or paternity leave.
- [ ] **Your working days are right.** Weekends are left out of leave days. To start with, the weekend is Saturday and Sunday.
- [ ] **People can log in.** Employees request leave in the employee portal or the Centa HR app.

## Bringing balances over from another system?

If people already have leave left this year, plan how you'll bring it over before they start requesting. [Contact support](/contact) with your current balances and we'll help you load them.
