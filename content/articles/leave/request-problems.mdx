CentaHR checks each request against your company's rules before it goes to your manager. If one isn't met, a message appears on the form straight away, and nothing is sent.

## Messages and what to do

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insufficient leave balance. You have … days left. | You're asking for more days than you have. | Ask for fewer days, or a different type such as unpaid leave. |
| You must apply for leave at least … days in advance. | Your company needs notice. | Choose later dates, or speak to your manager for something urgent. |
| You cannot take more than … consecutive leave days. | The request is longer than allowed in one go. | Split it into shorter requests, with a working day between. |
| Cannot request leave on blocked day | A date in your request is blocked for everyone. | Choose dates around it. Your HR team can tell you why it's blocked. |
| Cannot request leave on reserved day | A date in your request is set aside. | Choose other dates, or ask HR. |
| This leave must be taken as a continuous period. | This kind of leave can't be split. | Ask for all of it in one request. |
| Only confirmed employees can request this leave. | You're still on probation. | Until you're confirmed, you can request the types your company allows, such as sick leave. |
| Only … employees can request this leave. | This leave is for women only or for men only. | Check your gender on your profile is right, or ask HR. |
| No leave balance found for this leave type. | You don't have this kind of leave set up. | Ask HR to check you're eligible. |
| No effective leave days available for the requested period. | Every day you chose is a weekend or a holiday. | Check your dates. |
| You are not allowed to request this leave type. | This leave isn't available to you. | Ask HR which types you can request. |

Still stuck? Ask your HR team, or [contact support](/contact).
